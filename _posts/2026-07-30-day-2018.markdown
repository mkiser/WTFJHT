The Senate Judiciary Committee postponed its vote after Republican Sens. John Cornyn and Thom Tillis demanded written guarantees that the nearly $1.8 billion “Anti-Weaponization Fund” can't be revived and that the protections only cover past returns and the parties named in Trump’s lawsuit. Trump, meanwhile, said he had “no objection” to pulling Blanche’s nomination and trying again after the senators leave office in January. 2/ **Iran vowed to “punish the aggressor” after the U.S. launched a “heavy wave of strikes” against dozens of Iranian targets**. The strikes came in response to ballistic missiles Iran fired at American forces in Jordan. Central Command said the operation hit command centers, missile and drone facilities, coastal defenses, and maritime capabilities tied to Iran’s Revolutionary Guards. The war also widened to Egypt for the first time, where an unidentified drone set fire to two gas vessels at the Mediterranean port of Damietta, including a U.S.-owned gas storage tanker. Saudi Arabia, meanwhile, formed a 14-country maritime coalition intended to protect Red Sea shipping as attacks increasingly threaten the remaining energy and trade routes. 3/ **ICE expanded immigration arrests at airports beyond travelers with deportation orders to people who entered the country legally, but overstayed their visas**. Six immigration lawyers said some of those detained have pending asylum applications, work permits, or protections limiting removal to their home countries. The arrests appear to coincide with increased information sharing between ICE and the TSA. The Department of Homeland Security, meanwhile, said immigrants in the country illegally “can no longer fly unless it is out of our country to self-deport.” ([Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/dc-md-va/2026/07/30/ice-is-expanding-its-arrests-airports-new-targets-lawyers-say/))

4/ **The U.S. economy slowed to a 1.5% annualized growth rate in the second quarter**, down from 2.1%. The Federal Reserve’s preferred inflation measure, meanwhile, eased to 3.7% in June from 4.1% in May, but remains nearly twice the Fed’s 2% target. Core inflation was 3.3%, the personal savings rate fell to 2.7%, and renewed fighting with Iran pushed gasoline prices above $4 a gallon. Employers added 57,000 jobs in June, less than half May’s gain. Weekly unemployment claims rose to 197,000. ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/economy-inflation-spending-growth-consumers-growth-jobs-caf3b24d92688568f9c4f95725c87e55) / [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/30/business/economy/us-economy-gdp-growth.html) / [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Business/government-report-show-us-economy-performed-amid-iran/story?id=135152547) / [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/markets/us/us-inflation-slows-june-reversal-likely-amid-middle-east-conflict-2026-07-30/) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/07/30/economy-slowed-second-quarter-while-inflation-improved/) / [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/economy/gdp-slowed-second-quarter-rcna589992) / [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/07/30/nx-s1-5913230/economy-gdp-inflation) / [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/30/gdp-q2-economy-ai) / [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/unemployment-benefits-jobless-claims-layoffs-labor-99d765b2bbab7e278fb3eaed818d8319) / [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/us-weekly-jobless-claims-increase-less-than-expected-2026-07-30/))

5/ **The Energy Department admitted that it selected more than 600 clean-energy grants for cancellation “solely” because the projects were in states Kamala Harris won in 2024**. The Office of Management and Budget later terminated 284 of the grants in October 2025. The admission came from a class-action lawsuit brought by University of California researchers seeking to overturn the cancellations and restore funding. The Energy Department later denied that “any of the termination decisions were based on political considerations,” directly contradicting the department’s own federal court filing. ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/25/politics/trump-administration-grants-democratic-states))
