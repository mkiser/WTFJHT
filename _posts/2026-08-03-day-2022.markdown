Republican Sens. 2/ **Capital One closed more than 300 bank accounts linked to Trump and his businesses in 2021 after “months of analysis and a careful review” by its anti-money laundering team**. The bank said “transaction patterns” triggered the review, but didn't accuse the Trump Organization of money laundering. Trump’s trust, several of his companies, and Eric Trump sued Capital One, alleging the bank unlawfully shut down roughly 385 accounts for political reasons after the Jan. 6 attack and caused “considerable financial harm.” Capital One is seeking to have that case thrown out, saying it “never publicized the termination decision,” gave the account holders months and multiple extensions to find new banks, and that the closures became public only because Trump sued. ([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/capital-closed-hundreds-trumps-bank-accounts-2021-anti/story?id=135273257) / [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/03/business/capital-one-trump-org-money-laundering) / [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/trump-capital-one-debanking-money-laundering-5f5cc717e1e011e6195bd765c8f90bad))
