### 🍹 *While I was away*...

1/ **Trump signed an executive order to scale back the federal childhood vaccine schedule**. The order splits the measles, mumps and rubella vaccine into three shots, and spread childhood immunizations across separate doctor visits. It recommends routine vaccination against 11 diseases and directs the Justice Department to challenge some state vaccination laws. An earlier attempt to overhaul the schedule has already been blocked in federal court and legal experts say it’s unclear whether Trump even has the authority to impose the changes. Nevertheless, Trump claimed without evidence that the combined MMR vaccine could be “quite lethal” and again suggested vaccines contribute to autism, despite decades of research finding no link. The CDC also says there is “no published scientific evidence” of any benefit to separating the MMR vaccine. No individual measles, mumps, or rubella vaccines are currently approved or available in the U.S. ([New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/10/well/trump-vaccines-executive-order.html) / [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/10/trump-order-split-up-mmr-vaccine) / [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/health/kids-health/trump-order-fewer-childhood-vaccines-rcna591774) / [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/trump-sign-order-seeking-more-vaccine-flexibility-white-house-says-2026-08-10/) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/08/10/trump-administration-order-would-upend-nations-childhood-vaccine-schedule/))

2/ **Trump signed executive orders to narrow birthright citizenship and end “birth tourism,"** weeks after the Supreme Court ruled that his previous attempts to end automatic citizenship to children born in the U.S. were unconstitutional. One order would exclude children born to some foreign-government employees, people designated as “alien enemies” or members of foreign terrorist organizations, or mothers who entered the country solely to give birth. The other order directs the State Department and Homeland Security to issue rules and guidance targeting birth tourism, which federal visa rules already restrict. ([New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/06/us/politics/trump-birthright-citizenship-tourism.html) / [Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/08/06/trump-rolls-out-orders-intended-to-narrow-birthright-citizenship-and-target-birth-tourism-01028097) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/08/06/trump-tries-new-route-restrict-birthright-citizenship-after-loss/) / [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/06/g-s1-137686/trump-birthright-citizenship-immigration-curb) / [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/06/politics/trump-birthright-citizenship) / [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/06/trump-birth-tourism-birthright-citizenship))
