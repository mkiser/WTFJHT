1/ **Trump threatened to “bomb the shit out of” Oman if it “gets in the way” of U.S. efforts in the Strait of Hormuz**. The threat comes after Iran said it hd reached an “understanding” with Oman on a route map for ships through the strait. The U.S., however, has demanded that Iran reopen the strait first before it lifts its blockade of Iranian ports. Meanwhile, the U.S. and Iran missed the 60-day deadline for a peace deal. Trump ruled out extending the June agreement, which called for an end to military operations, the reopening of the strait, and negotiations over Iran’s nuclear program. Iran, however, claim that negotiations “never began” and the 60-day period was “fundamentally no longer relevant.” Trump, meanwhile, declared that he has “no time schedule” to end the war and is “not in a hurry” to make a deal. 2/ **Iran rejected Trump’s threat to declare the Strait of Hormuz U.S. territory**. On Friday, Trump claimed that he’d “pretty soon” declare the strait “a territory of the United States,” insisting that “no ships get through unless we want them to.” Early Saturday, Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi responded that the waterway “will only be opened and closed under Iran’s command,” adding that the U.S. must “accept the reality of defeat and stop indulging in delusions.” Gharibabadi added that the strait “cannot be seized with a tweet, nor with an aircraft carrier, nor by issuing an order, nor through an election speech.” ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/iran/iran-rejects-delusions-trump-strait-hormuz-us-territory-rcna592666) / [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/14/trump-threat-strait-hormuz-us-territory) / [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/international/6031248-iran-rejects-trump-hormuz-claim/) / [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2026/08/14/trump-straight-of-hormuz-territory-iran-war/91310185007/))

3/ **U.S. Central Command chief acknowledged mental health challenges aboard the U.S.S. Abraham Lincoln**. Adm. Brad Cooper claimed the carrier has “among the lowest number of cases related to mental health” of the Navy’s 11 active aircraft carriers after visiting. However, he acknowledged that “this doesn’t mean that all is perfect” and that serving at sea for long periods “isn’t for everyone.” Reports cited shortages, contaminated water, plumbing problems, deteriorating morale, and sailors attempting to go overboard during the extended deployment. Trump, meanwhile, dismissed concerns about the crew’s mental health and whether the deployment had gone on too long, responding “No. No. No.” and “Not nearly long enough.” ([New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/16/us/politics/uss-lincoln-iran-admiral.html) / [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-16/us-commander-says-mental-health-a-priority-aboard-uss-lincoln) / [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/16/top-us-commander-acknowledges-mental-health-issues-on-uss-lincoln.html))

7/ **The Democratic National Committee approved its 2028 presidential primary calendar with South Carolina going first**. South Carolina votes Jan. 22, followed by Nevada, New Hampshire, New Mexico, Michigan, and Virginia. The schedule drops Iowa from the early-state lineup and pushes New Hampshire to third, putting states with larger Black and Latino electorates at the front of the nominating process. New Hampshire Democrats, however, resisted the change in part because state law requires its primary be held before any other state. Some Iowa politicians, meanwhile, threatened to hold an early contest anyway. poll/ **60% of Americans called the U.S. economic system unfair**. 69% said they're dissatisfied or angry with how the system is working, 73% said the income gap between the richest Americans and the middle class is increasing, and 67% said wealthy Americans pay less than their fair share in federal income taxes. However, two-thirds said the free-market system is good for the economy, and capitalism was viewed more positively than socialism overall. Among Democrats, 58% viewed socialism positively, while 32% viewed capitalism positively. 53% also viewed the “democratic socialist” label positively. Meanwhile, 68% of Democrats want the party to pursue “big or fundamental changes” on economic policy even if they're harder to accomplish. 83% said it was more important to show they're “fighting for people” than to show they have a detailed plan, while 44% said the party is “not as progressive as you want it to be,” compared with 8% who said it is “more progressive than you want it to be.” ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/democrats-socialism-economy-political-parties-opinion-poll/))
