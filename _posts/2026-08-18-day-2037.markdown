1/ **Trump posted a map of the Strait of Hormuz that was labeled as “new U.S. territory."** He also declared that no talks were underway or scheduled with Iran, contradicting Jared Kushner, who said conversations with “different areas of the Iranian government” were ongoing and “probably more robust” than ever. Nevertheless, Trump claimed that the waterway was “open and operating” and that “all water mines have been removed or detonated” even though shipping remains a fraction of prewar levels. Iran, meanwhile, maintains that Hormuz will remain closed until the U.S. lifts its blockade of Iranian ports, removes sanctions, and ends military operations. ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-israel-lebanon-gaza-hormuz-august-18-2026-9c48af23b713709e8e170191fbc78c2a) / [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/trump-says-no-talks-planned-with-iran-tehran-says-strait-hormuz-still-shut-2026-08-18/) / [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/world/2026/08/18/trump-talks-iran-blockade/91351043007/) / [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/18/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-trump-ceasefire-expires-extension-.html) / [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/administration/6034751-trump-posts-map-labeling-strait-of-hormuz-as-us-territory/) / [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/International/live-updates/iran-live-updates-tehran-adopt-offensive-aspect-irgc/?id=135700072))

2/ **U.S. Customs and Border Protection paused all border security construction in Big Bend National Park**. Commissioner Rodney Scott ordered the pause until he could conduct a “personal evaluation” and meet with local officials, law enforcement, and other stakeholders after the project drew bipartisan opposition, protests, and lawsuits over bulldozing and excavating in parts of the protected landscape. Republican Sen. John Cornyn and local sheriffs argued that Big Bend’s rugged terrain already acts as a natural barrier in a region that accounts for about 1% of border apprehensions. CBP, nevertheless, has already begun clearing land in the park for patrol roads and vehicle barriers. ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-pauses-border-construction-big-bend-national-park-after-widespread-criticism-2026-08-17/) / [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/17/us/big-bend-border-security-halted) / [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/trump-administration-big-bend-national-park-construction-pause-rcna593005) / [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/17/trump-big-bend-border-wall-construction-paused) / [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/17/us/big-bend-border-wall-texas-construction.html))

* **The Forest Service proposed opening 44.7 million acres of undeveloped national forest to road construction and logging**, including nine million undeveloped acres in Alaska’s Tongass National Forest. Agriculture Secretary Brooke Rollins claimed that the restrictions have prevented forest management needed to reduce wildfire risk. ([New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/18/climate/trump-ends-roadless-rule-forests.html))

3/ **ABC and Disney sued the FCC to block its early review of eight ABC broadcast licenses**. In April, the FCC ordered an early review of the licenses while investigating Disney's diversity and inclusion practices. The order, however, came immediately after Trump attacked ABC and called for Jimmy Kimmel to be fired over a joke that Melania Trump had a “glow like an expectant widow.” Chairman Brendan Carr separately argued that ABC’s decision not to air Trump’s Oval Office address could be raised during the review. ABC called the early review an “existential threat.” The FCC denied that the review was retaliatory, arguing that broadcasters have a legal obligation to operate in the public interest. ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/abc-disney-sues-fcc-trump-administration-first-amendment-rcna593088) / [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/18/business/media/abc-fcc-lawsuit-broadcast-licenses.html) / [Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/08/18/abc-disney-fcc-license-lawsuit-carr-01039988) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/08/18/abc-sues-fcc-claiming-first-amendment-violations-escalating-months-long-feud/) / [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/18/abc-fcc-first-amendment-lawsuit))

4/ **The White House attacked CNN correspondent Kristen Holmes and her children after asking Trump to respond to Sen. Jon Ossoff’s criticism of him**. The White House’s “rapid response” account called Holmes a “disgraceful, humiliating embarrassment to her alleged profession” and told her that her children would someday be “sickened and embarrassed” by her question. Trump told Holmes to “be quiet,” called her “disrespectful” and a “fake reporter,” and told her “you report fake news” as she tried to ask another question. CNN, meanwhile, called personal attacks on journalists asking questions “beneath the office and inconsistent with the principles of a free press.” ([Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/08/17/white-house-attacks-cnn-reporter-after-trump-clash-targets-her-children/) / [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/18/us/politics/trump-lifeguard-cnn-reporter.html) / [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/17/white-house-personal-attack-cnn-reporter-trump) / [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/media/cnn-fires-back-trump-white-house-attacks-reporter-calls-beneath-office))

7/ **The Justice Department plans to send about 1,000 federal monitors to polling places for the November midterms**. Civil Rights Division chief Harmeet Dhillon called the deployment “probably going to be a historic number for a Republican administration.” While the department routinely sends monitors to enforce federal voting laws, an estimated 700 people were deployed for the 2024 presidential election and fewer than 300 for the 2022 midterms.
