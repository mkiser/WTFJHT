1/ **U.S. debt topped $40 trillion for the first time**. The Treasury Department said the total public debt outstanding reached $40.047 trillion Tuesday, more than double the level from a decade ago. Interest payments have topped $1 trillion this year and are now the government’s second-largest expense behind Social Security. Meanwhile, the Treasury has doubled the size of its buybacks of long-term government debt after 30-year yields reached their highest level in nearly two decades. The government ran a $432.3 billion deficit in July, the highest monthly shortfall in more than five years, as the deficit for the first 10 months of fiscal 2026 has already passed the total for all of fiscal 2025. Trump, who promised during his 2016 campaign to eliminate the national debt within eight years, has instead added roughly $11.6 trillion in additional debt across his two terms so far. 4/ **The Census Bureau published an unsigned analysis claiming that 24,000 noncitizens voted in 2020**. The report matched 128 million voter records against federal administrative records but relied on an incomplete commercial voter file that didn't include Social Security numbers, a key identifier for accurately matching records. The report also didn't identify which candidates the alleged voters supported. Trump, nevertheless, claimed the analysis “proves that over 24,000 Noncitizens voted illegally!” and declared, “I WON THE ELECTION!” ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/19/nx-s1-5938799/trump-census-bureau-noncitizen-voting) / [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/20/politics/flawed-census-report-alleged-noncitizen-voting) / [Democracy Docket](https://www.democracydocket.com/news-alerts/trump-census-bureau-revive-2020-election-lies-advance-attack-voting/))
