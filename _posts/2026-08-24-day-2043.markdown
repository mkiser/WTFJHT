5/ **The Supreme Court allowed Trump to move ahead with mail-voting restrictions**. The conservative majority concluded that California and 22 other states lacked standing because the order itself hadn't harmed them, yet. The 6-3 ruling addressed only whether the states' challenge was premature, not whether Trump had authority to impose the rules that directs the Department of Homeland Security to compile citizenship lists, requires states to send eligible-voter rosters to the Postal Service, and calls for mail ballots to be sent only to voters on those lists using special bar-coded envelopes. However, a separate nationwide injunction against parts of the same order remains in place. Meanwhile, the Postal Service finalized rules requiring states to submit voter data and barcodes through a federal portal and use special bar-coded election mail envelopes. The agency maintained that it's not deciding who is eligible to vote, checking citizenship, inspecting ballots, or counting votes. Trump has claimed the restrictions would combat voter fraud.
