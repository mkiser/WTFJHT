1/ **Canada retaliated against Trump’s new tariffs with duties of up to 50% on U.S. goods**. They take effect Sept. 8, and cover about $20 billion in American goods, doubling duties on U.S. steel and aluminum to 50% and imposing rates of 15% to 50% on hundreds of other products, including dairy, appliances, seafood, clothing, and electronics. The tariffs match Trump’s latest duties “dollar for dollar, rate for rate.” The move follows the collapse of trade talks and Trump’s imposition of 50% tariffs on about $20 billion of Canadian goods. 2/ **Trump threatened to rename Lake Ontario to “Lake America.”** Trump claimed he was giving the change “serious consideration” because “we don’t expect to be doing much business with Ontario any longer.” He later posted an altered map with “Lake Ontario” crossed out and “Lake America” in its place. However, Lake Ontario spans the U.S.-Canada border, and while Trump could change the name used by the federal government, Canada and international bodies wouldn't be required to recognize it. Canada-U.S. Trade Minister Dominic LeBlanc, meanwhile, said Canada had decided “not to respond to sort of the daily social media posts of either the president or his Cabinet secretaries.” ([Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/08/25/trump-says-us-could-rename-lake-ontario-amid-canada-trade-war/) / [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Politics/trump-floats-renaming-lake-ontario-lake-america-denies/story?id=135932301) / [Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/world/americas/trump-threatens-to-rename-lake-ontario-as-spat-with-canada-escalates-64a8b1b0) / [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/25/politics/trump-canada-lake-ontario-name) / [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/25/trump-french-language-canada-lake-ontario) / [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-lake-ontario-lake-america-canada-trade/))

5/ **🟥🟦 Primaries: Trump urged South Carolina Republicans to vote for interim Sen. Darline Graham over Rep. Ralph Norman in a special Senate runoff**, telling voters to “pretend, please, that I’m on the ballot.” Graham, the sister of the late Lindsey Graham, had never been elected to office before she was appointed to serve the remainder of his term. 6/ **The EPA proposed eliminating the federal requirement that states notify the public and solicit comments before issuing permits for data centers and other “minor” pollution sources**. The change would leave public participation largely to state and local discretion. The EPA claimed that the proposal would speed permitting but “does not alter emission standards or weaken environmental protections,” while nearly 200 advocacy groups and Democratic attorneys general from 14 states opposed it. Resistance to data centers, meanwhile, has spread across party lines: Texas Gov. Greg Abbott paused new projects pending an audit, Republican Senate candidate Ken Paxton proposed new restrictions, and Pennsylvania Gov. Josh Shapiro required developers to craft “detailed community outreach plans.” ([The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/25/datacenters-air-pollution-epa) / [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/25/climate/epa-data-centers-public-comment.html) / [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/25/climate-power-memo-data-centers-democrats-elections) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/nation/2026/08/24/ken-paxton-unveils-anti-data-center-plan-gop-voter-anger-mounts-texas/))
