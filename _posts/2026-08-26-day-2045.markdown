1/ **A federal judge lifted the final nationwide injunction blocking Trump’s mail-voting order**. The decision allows the Postal Service to impose new ballot requirements ahead of the November midterms, requiring election officials to provide mail-voter information, use federally reviewed ballot envelopes with unique barcodes, and correct and resubmit mailings that fail verification. Judge Indira Talwani lifted her injunction after the Supreme Court ruled [this week](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/08/24/day-2043/#5-the-supreme-court-allowed-trump-to) that a related challenge was premature. Neither ruling, however, decided whether the restrictions are legal. The states argued that the requirements exceed federal authority and could disenfranchise voters by forcing election officials to redesign ballot envelopes, build new systems, train workers, and educate voters as some states prepare to mail ballots in early September. 3/ **The annual U.S. inflation rose 3.7% in July, the same as in June, but well above the Fed’s 2% target**. Core inflation, which excludes food and energy, was also unchanged at 3.3%. Meanwhile, consumer spending was flat after adjusting for inflation. Spending on goods fell by about $50 billion at an annualized rate, while spending on services increased by $86 billion. 4/ **Trump called Canada “one of the worst countries” in the world to deal with**, saying it's “time to teach Canada you can’t do this anymore.” Trump accused Canada of taking advantage of the U.S. “as though they were a state,” adding that “they don’t have anything that we have to have.” After Trump imposed 50% tariffs on $20 billion in Canadian imports following the collapse of trade talks, Canada retaliated with matching tariffs on about $20 billion in U.S. goods starting Sept. 8. Trump also announced 50% tariffs on Canadian autos and parts beginning Jan. 1. Nevertheless, Trump claimed he had offered Canada “a pretty good deal,” but later appeared to acknowledge Canada’s claim that the U.S. added new conditions in the final hours before negotiations broke down. 6/ **A federal judge struck down a 2023 Texas law that banned drag shows**. For a second time, U.S. District Judge David Hittner ruled that the law violates the First Amendment and was so broad that its ban on performances appealing to a “prurient interest in sex” could be applied to cheerleading, live theater, and performances by Dolly Parton (RIP), Elvis Presley, and Miley Cyrus to civil or criminal penalties. “For those who find such activities [...] offensive,” Hittner wrote, “the solution is relatively simple [...] just don’t go.” ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/politics-news/judge-strikes-texas-ban-drag-shows-tells-people-offended-just-dont-go-rcna594448) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/nation/2026/08/26/judge-channels-dolly-parton-blocking-texas-drag-show-ban/) / [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/26/texas-drag-performance-ban-fails) / [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/nation/2026/08/26/texas-drag-ban-ruling/91470326007/))
