The 5-4 unsigned decision blocked lower-court rulings that would have stopped aboveground work, concluding that the National Trust for Historic Preservation likely lacks standing. However, Chief Justice John Roberts joined the court’s three liberal justices in dissent, writing that the construction is “likely unlawful” because Congress hasn't expressly authorized the project. 2/ **Trump vowed to “hit them hard” after Iran fired missiles at U.S. bases in Jordan and launched a drone toward the United Arab Emirates**. It was the first direct exchange of attacks in about a month. U.S. forces struck two Iranian rocket launchers on Larak Island after CENTCOM reported that Revolutionary Guard forces were preparing to deploy sea mines into the Strait of Hormuz. Iran then fired missiles at the bases in Jordan and launched a drone toward the United Arab Emirates. Trump, however, later said the U.S. response would be “very limited.” ([New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/31/world/middleeast/us-iran-uae-jordan-attacks.html) / [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/trump-posts-ai-video-irans-kharg-smithereens-no-evidence-attack-2026-08-31/) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2026/08/30/us-strikes-iran-missile-sites-larak-island/) / [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/national-security/us-forces-strike-iranian-rocket-launchers-strait-hormuz-first-military-rcna595194) / [Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/08/31/donald-trump-iran-strikes-01057142) / [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/world/2026/08/31/iran-war-trump-threatens-strikes/91545559007/) / [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/30/politics/us-iran-strikes-larak-island) / [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-30/us-strikes-iranian-rocket-launchers-in-first-attack-in-weeks) / [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/30/us-iran-strikes-strait-hormuz.html) / [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/30/us-iran-strike-hormuz-mines))

3/ **The Pentagon installed several conservative military influencers in undisclosed civilian government roles**. Pentagon records showed that one retired Air Force colonel and one retired Army colonel had active civilian government email addresses assigned to the office of the undersecretary of defense for personnel and readiness. A third retired colonel was also listed there earlier this year. All three also appeared on behalf of a task force reviewing senior military schools for ideology deemed “toxic” or “woke,” and publicly amplified Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth’s views and attacked critics of the Trump administration. ([Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2026/08/30/pentagon-secretly-installs-military-influencers-civilian-roles/))
