1/ **The Supreme Court denied Missouri’s request to use a Republican-drawn congressional map for the November midterm election designed to give Republicans an additional seat in the U.S. House**. Justice Brett Kavanaugh’s order left intact a Missouri Supreme Court ruling requiring the state to return to its 2022 districts while voters decide in a referendum whether to approve the new map. Minutes later, however, U.S. District Judge Stephen Clark temporarily blocked Missouri from using anything but the new map, writing that switching districts after the August primary would “wreak havoc on the fairness and orderliness of Missouri’s congressional elections” and that “widespread voter confusion would inevitably follow.” Missouri used the new districts in its August primary, making it unclear which map will be used in the November election. ([Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/09/08/supreme-court-rejects-missouri-redistricting-bid-01068211) / [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/08/us/politics/supreme-court-missouri-congressional-map.html) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/09/08/supreme-court-blocks-missouri-congressional-map-favoring-gop/) / [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/supreme-court/rejects-missouri-republicans-request-use-new-congressional-map-midterm-rcna596454) / [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/08/politics/supreme-court-missouri-redistricting-loss-for-republicans) / [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/redistricting-congress-trump-gerrymandering-house-aadd3c4e6cc3a2c8100756cb7b352289))

2/ **The Trump administration asked the Supreme Court for a third time to revive its mail-ballot restrictions**. The request came two days after a federal judge blocked the rules from taking effect before the November midterms, which would require states and local election officials to provide lists of mail voters and use special barcodes on ballot envelopes. Judge Indira Talwani, however, ruled that the policy likely violates the Constitution’s separation of powers, which gives states and Congress responsibility over elections. Talwani also warned that implementing it less than 70 days before the election “threatens disenfranchisement of millions of United States citizens who seek to vote by mail.” The Trump administration, meanwhile, argued that the rule “does not seize control of States’ administration of elections” and instead imposes “reasonable preparation requirements for certain election-related mail.” ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/03/nx-s1-5955614/trump-mail-in-voting-ruling-court-usps) / [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/06/us/politics/trump-mail-in-voting-supreme-court.html) / [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/trump-administration/trump-administration-supreme-court-mail-ballot-restrictions-appeal-rcna596346) / [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/trump-mail-voting-executive-order-lawsuit-65067233db6ebda8dedcdc3f6714a96c) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/09/06/trump-is-running-out-time-he-tries-restrict-mail-voting/) / [Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/09/06/trump-supreme-court-mail-in-voting-01066759) / [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-supreme-court-appeal-us-postal-service-mail-ballot-fight/) / [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/06/politics/trump-administration-again-asks-supreme-court-to-allow-mail-ballot-rule-to-take-effect) / [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/sep/06/trump-administration-supreme-court-mail-in-voting))

3/ **The Trump administration asked the Supreme Court to let states check their voter rolls against federal citizenship and Social Security data**. In June, a federal judge blocked the SAVE system, finding that the government had “haphazardly combined and repurposed” unreliable citizenship data and that states had removed U.S. citizens from voter rolls based on inaccurate information. The Trump administration, nevertheless, asked the Supreme Court to restore access to the system, which had been expanded to include Social Security data and bulk searches. Solicitor General D. John Sauer called the order “indefensible” and argued that it “threatens the integrity of upcoming elections” by making it harder for states to verify citizenship. ([Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/09/08/trump-asks-supreme-court-to-restore-states-access-to-citizenship-database-before-election) / [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/08/us/politics/trump-supreme-court-dhs-citizenship.html) / [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-08/supreme-court-asked-to-enter-voter-verification-database-fight) / [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/trump-asks-supreme-court-allow-voter-verification-database-blocked-by-judge-2026-09-08/) / [Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/politics/policy/trump-officials-ask-high-court-to-allow-immigration-database-for-voter-scrutiny-a7197aad) / [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/08/politics/trump-supreme-court-citizenship-voter-purge-save) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/09/08/dhs-asks-supreme-court-ok-use-social-security-data-verify-voting-eligibility/) / [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/supreme-court-trump-save-system-database/))

4/ **U.S. forces destroyed five Iranian oil tankers after Iran’s Revolutionary Guards targeted a Navy warship with ballistic missiles twice over the past two days**. Central Command said the warship evaded the attacks and no American personnel were harmed. The strikes comes three days after the U.S. destroyed three other Iranian tankers following attempted missile attacks on an American aircraft carrier and destroyer.
