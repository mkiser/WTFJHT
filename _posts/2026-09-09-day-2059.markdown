The 14th Amendment, however, calls for House seats to be apportioned by counting the “whole number of persons in each State.” The proposal would also prohibit race and ethnicity questions on the decennial census and ban questions about sexual orientation, changes that could affect redistricting, enforcement of anti-discrimination laws, and the distribution of federal funds. 3/ **A federal appeals court in Louisiana heard arguments over the state’s effort to restrict mail-order mifepristone nationwide**, claiming that the FDA’s Biden-era rules allowing telehealth prescriptions and mail delivery make its abortion ban “meaningless” because patients can just have the drugs shipped across state lines. The state asked the court to eliminate the federal rules nationwide, even in states where abortion is legal. Meanwhile, in a friend-of-the-court brief, 22 Democratic attorneys general and the District of Columbia argued that Louisiana “improperly elevates the policy preferences of states that have banned or restricted abortion over the preferences of other States that have made the different but equally sovereign determinations to promote access to abortion care.” Medication abortion now accounts for two-thirds of U.S. abortions. 7/ **Trump claimed two firefighters carried him to safety near Ground Zero during a 9/11 tribute**. He also claimed he brought a construction crew to help “right after” the attacks. However, there is no public evidence corroborating either story, and Trump was reportedly four miles away at Trump Tower on Sept. 11. In a 2016 version of the firefighter story, he said the incident happened “a couple of days” later. Videos also show Trump was in Lower Manhattan on Sept. 13, 2001, when he claimed to have about 100 workers helping with the recovery, but a former fire official said he never saw Trump there and that a crew that large would have left a record. Nevertheless, Trump later clarified that he was “obviously not on the same day as the collapse, but shortly thereafter” and that “everything I said was exactly accurate.” ([New York Magazine](https://nymag.com/intelligencer/article/trump-repeats-disputed-yarn-about-his-9-11-role.html) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/09/08/honoring-911-heroes-trump-repeats-disputed-account-his-role-recovery/) / [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/donald-trump/firefighters-ground-zero-right-after-september-11-attacks-rcna596723) / [New Republic](https://newrepublic.com/post/215196/trump-reaction-fact-checks-911-lie) / [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/08/politics/fact-check-trump-9-11-claims) / [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/08/us/politics/trump-sept-11-firefighters.html))
