1/ **The Trump administration removed about 760,000 people from Affordable Care Act coverage**, claiming they were improperly enrolled or didn't exist. Another roughly 419,000 enrollees will face additional eligibility verification. JD Vance said that canceling their federal subsidies would save $2.2 billion. The administration also imposed a six-month moratorium on new insurance brokers selling ACA plans. Mehmet Oz, the administrator of the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services, meanwhile, said the enrollees facing additional verification had “something about their story that was a little bit different than what we expected from the 100 percent fraud story,” adding: “We’re giving them another chance.” ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/vance-healthcare-oz-aca-fraud-task-force-eb57a4819c8a876a72c1301a112e21dc) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/09/22/trump-officials-plan-cut-hundreds-thousands-aca-claiming-fraud/) / [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/trump-administration-to-remove-750-000-from-obamacare-rolls) / [Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/politics/policy/vance-led-task-force-set-to-remove-760-000-allegedly-fraudulent-obamacare-enrollments-0fbb72d7) / [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/22/health/aca-obamacare-vance-fraud.html) / [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/healthcare/6104381-vance-oz-aca-obamacare-crackdown/))

2/ **Trump threatened to "annihilate" Iran**, claiming that he faces "a big decision" between allowing Iran to "rebuild and create a far greater country" or doing it "quickly." Hours later, however, Trump said U.S. and Iranian officials held a "very good" and "very productive" three-hour meeting about ending the nearly seven-month war, and that another meeting was planned "in the very near future." Iranian state television confirmed that Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi met with U.S. envoy Steve Witkoff and that Iran’s conditions for reopening the Strait of Hormuz included lifting the U.S. naval blockade, releasing frozen Iranian assets, and ending the war on all fronts. Trump later described Iran’s choice as "potential greatness or obliteration." 3/ **Trump told CNN’s Kaitlan Collins she “should not be here covering me”at the United Nations**, days after banning CNN, MS NOW, and Politico from the White House. Collins was credentialed by the United Nations, and had asked about polling that shows two-thirds of Americans don’t think the U.S. is winning the war with Iran. Trump said he was “surprised” and claimed that the network had said it wouldn’t cover him. Collins, however, denied that, saying CNN “never said we were not going to cover the president.” CNN, MS NOW, and Politico sued Trump and other administration officials Monday, alleging the White House ban violates the First Amendment and due process. ([Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/09/trump-cnn-ban-kaitlan-collins-un-1237110017/) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/09/22/trump-berates-cnns-kaitlan-collins-after-ban-you-shouldnt-be-covering-me/) / [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/22/trump-cnn-un-white-house-media-ban.html) / [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/22/trump-cnn-white-house-un-speech) / [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/administration/6103823-trump-collins-white-house-un-assembly/))

5/ **Trump renamed artificial intelligence “super intelligence” for U.S. government documents** days after saying he's [creating](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/09/21/day-2071/#5-trump-will-create-an-%E2%80%9Cai-force%E2%80%9D-th) an “AI Force.” During his speech to the United Nations General Assembly, Trump claimed “artificial” makes the technology “sound fake,” adding: “From this point forward, all of United States documents, and hopefully the world’s, will be changed to use the much more accurate term ‘super’ as opposed to ‘artificial.’” It’s unclear when or how the change will be implemented. Trump also rejected efforts to slow AI development. “I’m not going to stifle growth of something that will be bigger than the Industrial Revolution,” Trump said, while repeating his claim that AI safety warnings are a hoax. “We will only encourage super intelligence,” Trump added. ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/22/trump-ai-super-intelligence-rebrand) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/09/22/trump-says-hes-renaming-ai-super-intelligence/) / [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/trump-un-threats-diplomacy-super-intelligence-2026-09-22/))
