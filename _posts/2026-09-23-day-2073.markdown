1/ **Iran vowed to fight "until our last breath" and “never bow our head or bend at the knee” to Trump’s demands**. Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian, speaking at the United Nations General Assembly, declared that Iran was ready to negotiate with the U.S., but wouldn't give up its civilian nuclear program or accept what he called Washington’s “language of force.” His address came a day after Trump threatened to “annihilate” Iran if the nearly seven-month war ends without a deal. 2/ **The Justice Department urged a federal judge to uphold Trump’s ban of CNN, MS NOW, and Politico from the White House, arguing that “access to the White House is a privilege – not a right.”** The administration claimed the outlets violated “standards of professionalism and decorum” by “trafficking in verifiable falsehoods about national security and other issues, and publishing sensitive or classified information.” The White House cited reporting about the Iran war, U.S. munitions, and White House construction and security as justification for the ban. The Justice Department also asked the courts to overturn a 49-year-old precedent protecting journalists’ access to the White House.([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/news-media-ban-cnn-white-house-access-a3471270b8db89f6067c54528ed0a340) / [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/23/business/trump-cnn-politico-ms-now-ban.html) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/09/23/trump-tells-judge-media-ban-is-necessary-protect-national-security/) / [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Politics/doj-defends-white-house-news-bans-access-privilege/story?id=136674586) / [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-23/doj-tells-judge-trump-white-house-media-ban-is-lawful) / [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/22/politics/trump-admin-responds-press-lawsuit-white-house-access) / [Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/09/23/trump-press-ban-brief) / [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/doj-defends-trump-white-house-media-ban/) / [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/23/trump-white-house-ban-media-doj-cnn-msnow-politico.html) / [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/politics-news/trump-media-ban-white-house-access-privilege-national-security-doj-rcna599347))

3/ **The FBI was hacked and “very sensitive data” on nearly all agents and job applicants was stolen**, including names, home addresses, Social Security numbers, assignments and, in some cases, information about family members. The bureau hasn't determined "the point of breach" and whether the information came from FBI systems or a third-party provider. ShinyHunters, meanwhile, demanded that the FBI revise or remove a May advisory describing the group as specializing in large-scale data breaches and extortion. ([404 Media](https://www.404media.co/we-hacked-the-fbi-hackers-say-they-have-data-on-all-fbi-employees/) / [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/23/us/politics/fbi-hack-shinyhunters-data.html) / [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/hacking-group-shinyhunters-claims-it-breached-the-fbi-stole-agents-and-applicants-data/) / [Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/09/22/shinyhunters-fbi-cyber-hack-01088494) / [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/fbi-criminal-hacking-group-jobs-website-beach-d627cbc6811344e7ad41e9f164cdf1c9) / [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/shinyhunters-hackers-say-they-breached-federal-bureau-investigation-no-immediate-2026-09-22/) / [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/22/politics/fbi-investigating-stolen-agents-data-hackers) / [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/wireStory/fbi-investigates-apparent-breach-jobs-website-hackers-claim-136686678))

4/ **Seventeen Democratic senators urged Trump to seek an AI agreement with China to establish “guardrails” around development**. The senators argued that “AI technology is too powerful, and its risks too many, for the U.S. and China to forgo discussions,” and urged Trump to discuss slowing or pausing development with Chinese leader Xi Jinping. However, earlier this week, Trump declared that “I’m not going to stifle growth of anything that’s going to be bigger than the Industrial Revolution.” ([Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/09/23/dems-urge-trump-to-seek-ai-deal-with-china-01089253) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/09/23/trump-xi-jinping-will-talk-about-ai-they-arent-close-deal/))

5/ **The first architect for Trump’s White House ballroom resigned after warning that the preliminary design violated building safety codes**. Trump reportedly responded that no building codes apply to White House construction, because “I am the code.” White House records, however, show officials continued to discuss life-safety issues, like adequate emergency exits and fire-containment measures, after James McCrery II resigned. ([Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/investigations/2026/09/23/i-am-code-trump-clashed-with-ballroom-architect-over-safety-issues/))

* **Trump administration officials have discussed attaching Trump’s name to Ford’s Theatre**. The federal historic site where Abraham Lincoln was assassinated is owned by the Interior Department, and its name is set by federal law, meaning a formal renaming would require an act of Congress. ([MS Now](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-fords-theatre-name-kennedy-center))
