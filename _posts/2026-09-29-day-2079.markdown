1/ **The Supreme Court temporarily allowed the Trump administration to resume deporting migrants to “third countries” where they have no connections**. The unsigned order paused a lower-court ruling that said migrants were entitled to notice and a meaningful chance to raise fears of torture or persecution before deportation. More than 25,000 people have been deported to third countries under the policy. The justices will hear arguments in December on whether the policy is legal. 2/ **Former special counsel Jack Smith told the Senate Judiciary Committee that he stood by his decision to charge Trump and “will not be silenced” by threats of prosecution**. “Our investigation developed proof beyond a reasonable doubt that President Trump engaged in serious crimes against our nation,” Smith said. He added that he would've prosecuted any former president on the same facts “regardless of whether that President was a Republican or a Democrat.” Smith's investigations led to criminal indictments against Trump for trying to overturn the 2020 election and his mishandling of classified documents. Both cases were dropped after Trump’s 2024 election win under Justice Department policy barring prosecution of a sitting president. Committee Chairman Chuck Grassley, meanwhile, called Smith’s investigation “a partisan weapon that broke the rules,” while Sen. John Kennedy told him, “You make me want to throw up in my mouth.” ([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Politics/former-special-counsel-jack-smith-testify-senate-trump/story?id=136828779) / [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/jack-smith-trump-congress-5739c9cd8bee9652bdeedbd10336492b) / [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/29/us/politics/jack-smith-congress-testimony.html) / [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/former-trump-prosecutor-jack-smith-faces-senate-hearing-over-abuse-authority-2026-09-29/) / [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/29/politics/live-news/jack-smith-testifies-senate) / [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/justice-department/senate-gop-press-jack-smith-trump-jan-6-probe-doj-targets-investigator-rcna600306) / [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/jack-smith-trump-senate-judiciary-committee-hearing/) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/09/29/jack-smith-clashes-with-gop-senators-over-trump-investigations/) / [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-29/ex-trump-prosecutor-spars-with-gop-senators-defends-probe-staff))

3/ **A lawyer who tried to overturn the 2020 election resigned from the Justice Department's "grand conspiracy" investigation**. Kurt Olsen, a Trump ally and election denier, worked on the investigation into former federal officials. His departure follows the resignation of Joseph diGenova, who had been overseeing the investigation trying to establish that Obama- and Biden-era officials had engaged in a long-running criminal conspiracy Trump to keep him out of office. The investigation produced no charges. 6/ **Consumer confidence fell to its lowest level since 2014** as Americans grew more worried about prices and jobs. The Conference Board’s index dropped 6.7 points to 81.9 in September, and consumers’ expectations for the next six months also declined. For the first time in the four years, more respondents rated their personal finances bad than good. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/29/consumer-optimism-slides-sharply-as-fears-escalate-over-rising-prices-and-jobs.html) / [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-29/us-consumer-confidence-plunges-to-lowest-level-since-2014) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/09/29/americans-feel-worse-about-economy-than-during-pandemic/))
