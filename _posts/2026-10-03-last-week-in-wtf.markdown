**Last week in one sentence:** The Supreme Court temporarily allowed the Trump administration to resume deporting migrants to “third countries” where they have no connections; Trump's taxpayer-funded TV ads were paid for with $20 million in border security funding; Trump canceled $810 million in spending approved by Congress; the U.S. military formally withdrew from Iraq and ended its 12-year anti-ISIS operation; and the Supreme Court allowed the Trump administration to use a federal citizenship database to verify voter rolls ahead of the midterms.

### The Week, Distilled

1/ **The Supreme Court temporarily allowed the Trump administration to resume deporting migrants to “third countries” where they have no connections**. The unsigned order paused a lower-court ruling that migrants must be given notice and a meaningful chance to raise fears of torture or persecution before deportation. The Department of Homeland Security says more than 25,000 people have been deported to third countries under the policy. The justices will hear arguments in December on whether the policy is legal. ([Day 2078](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/09/28/day-2078/) / [Day 2079](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/09/29/day-2079/))

2/ **Trump's taxpayer-funded TV ads were paid for with $20 million in border security funding**. The White House moved money Congress had allocated for border surveillance and drug-trafficking enforcement into a Homeland Security account for “commemorative events.” DHS then awarded a $20 million “national media campaign” contract the next day. Federal law prohibits using public funds for “propaganda purposes,” but FCC Chair Brendan Carr called the ads “normal” public service announcements. ([Day 2079](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/09/29/day-2079/) / [Day 2081](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/01/day-2081/))

3/ **Trump canceled $810 million in spending approved by Congress**. The White House notified Congress five days before the fiscal year ended on Sept. 30, leaving lawmakers without the required 45-day review period. Senate Appropriations Chair Susan Collins called the move illegal, saying the White House budget office "does not get to decide which programs are worth funding." ([Day 2078](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/09/28/day-2078/))

4/ **The U.S. military formally withdrew from Iraq and ended its 12-year anti-ISIS operation**. Iraq is now responsible for containing ISIS and Iran-backed militias, while the Pentagon will continue to provide targeted training and intelligence support. It’s the second time the U.S. has withdrawn all of its military forces from Iraq since the 2003 invasion. ([Day 2080](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/09/30/day-2080/))

5/ **The Supreme Court allowed the Trump administration to use a federal citizenship database to verify voter rolls ahead of the midterms**. The order pauses a lower-court ruling that had blocked the system, which combines immigration and Social Security records to check voters’ citizenship status. Federal law, however, prohibits mass removal of voters within 90 days of Election Day. The emergency ruling was unsigned, with the court’s three liberal justices dissenting. ([Day 2078](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/09/28/day-2078/))

- **The Pentagon is preparing to send 10,000 more troops and a third aircraft carrier strike group to the Middle East**. The USS Theodore Roosevelt and a Marine amphibious group set sail this week, but one official said no final decision had been made on the carrier's destination.

- **The average 30-year fixed mortgage rate jumped to 7.28%, its highest since November 2023**. Rates rose from 7.03% last week, which was the biggest weekly increase since October 2022.
