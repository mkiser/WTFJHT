Separately, Trump renewed his promise to send every adult U.S. citizen $5,000 if Republicans retain control of Congress. However, he hasn't said how he plans to fund the roughly $1.2 trillion proposal, which requires congressional approval. Meanwhile, the administration began distributing $500 checks to roughly 1 million Affordable Care Act enrollees.
