While Trump has not made a final decision and ruled out strikes before the Nov. 3 midterms, the options under discussion include limited strikes and large-scale attacks on Iranian energy, infrastructure, and nuclear targets. Last week, Trump said he believed the U.S. would win the war after the election, adding “Maybe before the election.” At a campaign rally in San Antonio on Wednesday, Trump said a deal with Iran “isn’t really something that I want to do,” but claimed Iran was “willing to offer us anything to stop.” Negotiations, meanwhile, have made no progress. He didn't provide evidence that workers had been replaced. Microsoft, however, said that 80% of its roughly 6,000 H-1B applications last fiscal year were for existing employees. “These were not to hire new people,” the company said. 4/ **Trump made his third trip to Texas in less than a month to campaign for Republican Senate candidate Ken Paxton, who is trailing Democrat James Talarico in recent polls ahead of the midterms**. Trump won Texas by more than 13 points in 2024, but Republicans are now spending heavily to defend a state that hasn't elected a Democratic U.S. senator since 1988. Trump praised Paxton as a "fantastic man" and warned that a vote for Talarico would turn Texas into a "dirty, failing, crime-ridden third-world dumping ground." Paxton, however, was recorded privately saying the Iran war and high fuel prices were hurting his campaign and that Trump's midterm convention in Dallas "dropped our numbers." In another leaked recording, Paxton called for "reasonable cuts to mandatory spending" to reduce the federal deficit without identifying which programs he would cut. About three-quarters of mandatory federal spending goes to Social Security, Medicare, and Medicaid. 7/ **The Pentagon will livestream the firing squad execution of Nidal Malik Hasan**. Trump approved the Dec. 3 execution of Hasan, who was convicted of killing 13 people and injuring 32 others in the 2009 shooting. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth called the firing squad "as it should be" and said "We'll make sure that people are able to watch it, that it's public, because people need to understand that there's serious consequences for these types of things." If carried out, it would be the first U.S. military execution since 1961.
