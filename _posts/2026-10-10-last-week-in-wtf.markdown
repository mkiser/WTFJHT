**Last week in one sentence:** The Pentagon instructed U.S. Central Command to prepare to resume major combat operations in Iran; Trump made his third trip to Texas in less than a month to campaign for Republican Senate candidate Ken Paxton, who is trailing Democrat James Talarico in recent polls ahead of the midterms; the Trump administration indefinitely suspended Microsoft and seven other technology firms from using the H-1B visa program that allows foreign workers to seek permanent residency in the U.S; Trump's super PAC doesn't plan to reimburse taxpayers for the more than $10 million spent on TV ads promoting him; Trump plans to send $90 to 20.8 million Medicare beneficiaries to help offset their Part B premiums; Supreme Court Justice Samuel Alito considered retiring this year, but decided to stay so he could make "a valuable contribution"; and ICE has investigated more than 150 voter-registration groups ahead of the midterm elections.

---

1/ **The Pentagon instructed U.S. Central Command to prepare to resume major combat operations in Iran**. Trump hasn't made a final decision, but said the U.S. would not attack before the midterm elections. The options under discussion include limited strikes and large-scale attacks on Iranian energy, infrastructure, and nuclear targets. Trump claimed the U.S. and Iran were having “productive discussions,” although negotiations have made no progress and he said a deal “isn’t really something that I want to do.” Trump said the U.S. naval blockade of Iranian oil exports would remain in force as the military sends another aircraft carrier and thousands of troops to the region. ([Day 2085](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/05/day-2085/) / [Day 2086](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/06/day-2086/) / [Day 2088](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/08/day-2088/))

2/ **Trump made his third trip to Texas in less than a month to campaign for Republican Senate candidate Ken Paxton, who is trailing Democrat James Talarico in recent polls ahead of the midterms**. Republicans have spent more than $200 million on the race in a state Trump won by more than 13 points in 2024. Trump called Paxton a “fantastic man,” but Paxton was recorded privately blaming the Iran war and high fuel prices for his campaign’s struggles and saying Trump’s midterm convention in Dallas “dropped our numbers.” In another leaked recording, Paxton called for “reasonable cuts to mandatory spending” without saying which programs he would cut; about three-quarters of that spending goes to Social Security, Medicare, and Medicaid. ([Day 2087](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/07/day-2087/) / [Day 2088](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/08/day-2088/))

3/ **The Trump administration indefinitely suspended Microsoft and seven other technology firms from using the H-1B visa program that allows foreign workers to seek permanent residency in the U.S.** JD Vance accused Microsoft of visa fraud and claimed the program had become “rife” with fraud, but did not provide evidence that American workers had been replaced. Microsoft said 80% of its roughly 6,000 H-1B applications last fiscal year were for existing employees, “not to hire new people.” Hours later, Trump awarded Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella, a former H-1B visa holder, the National Medal of Technology and Innovation, calling the honor “so well deserved.” ([Day 2088](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/08/day-2088/))

4/ **Trump's super PAC doesn't plan to reimburse taxpayers for the more than $10 million spent on TV ads promoting him**. The White House said Trump's promise to pay for the “Patriotic Ads” with money raised for MAGA Inc. applies only to ads “moving forward” and “was not referring to reimbursement.” Asked whether he would repay the government for ads already aired, Trump said: “We’ll decide.” The ads aired under a $20 million Department of Homeland Security contract, and one reused footage from a 2024 Trump campaign video. The White House denied that the ads were political. ([Day 2086](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/06/day-2086/))

5/ **Trump plans to send $90 to 20.8 million Medicare beneficiaries to help offset their Part B premiums**. The one-time payments are expected before the Nov. 3 midterm elections and would come from the roughly $2 billion Medicare Improvement Fund. The standard Part B premium rose nearly 10% this year to $202.90 a month, despite Trump’s claim that he had reduced premiums “by significant amounts.” Trump also renewed his promise to send every adult U.S. citizen $5,000 if Republicans retain control of Congress, but hasn’t said how he would fund the roughly $1.2 trillion proposal, which requires congressional approval. The administration, meanwhile, began distributing $500 checks to roughly 1 million Affordable Care Act enrollees. ([Day 2085](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/05/day-2085/))

6/ **Supreme Court Justice Samuel Alito considered retiring this year, but decided to stay so he could make "a valuable contribution."** Alito, 76, said presidential politics should not dictate when he leaves because it is “too hard to predict what the political situation will be.” If he retires in 2027, Trump could nominate a fourth Supreme Court justice. Democrats have threatened to block Trump’s judicial nominees if they win the Senate in November, and Sen. Richard Blumenthal said the chances of a Democratic-led Senate confirming a Trump Supreme Court nominee are “slim to none.” ([Day 2085](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/05/day-2085/))

7/ **ICE has investigated more than 150 voter-registration groups ahead of the midterm elections**. The agency has also considered deploying undercover agents to investigate groups suspected of helping noncitizens register to vote. Internal Department of Homeland Security documents show ICE collected nearly 100 million records in its effort to identify noncitizens illegally registering or voting, including 48.4 million supplied by senior DHS adviser Heather Honey from unidentified “independent sources of information.” Honey has promoted false claims about the 2020 election. Noncitizen voting is rare, and there is no credible evidence that it has changed the outcome of an election. ([Day 2087](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/07/day-2087/))

### 📊 The Dept. of Numbers.

- **59% of Latino voters prefer a Democratic-controlled Congress**, compared with 35% who favor Republican control. 25% of Latinos approve of the job Trump is doing as president, while 69% disapprove.

- **The primary Senate Republican super PAC plans to spend $5 million to defend Sen. Darline Graham in South Carolina**. Trump won the state by about 18 points in 2024.

- **Kimberly Guilfoyle reportedly urged a Trump donor to pay her $100,000 American Express bill**. “It won’t show up anywhere if you wire money to American Express,” Trump Jr.’s former fiancée texted Eric Deters days before her Senate confirmation hearing as ambassador to Greece. "Honey please I need you to get this done for me today,” she added.

- **86% of Americans disapprove of using taxpayer money for the Trump's $20 million campaign-style TV ads**, while 9% support it.

- **A former CIA official pleaded guilty to wire fraud after diverting about $194 million in government funds, which he used to buy Florida mansions and gold**. David Rush also admitted in his plea agreement that he disclosed “certain descriptive information of a U.S. government clandestine human source” that could “endanger the safety and life of the human source and the U.S. government officers working with the source.” He faces up to 20 years in prison.
