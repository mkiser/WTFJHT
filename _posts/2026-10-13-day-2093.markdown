3/ **The Supreme Court agreed to hear the Trump administration's effort to withhold DOGE records**. Citizens for Responsibility and Ethics in Washington sued under the Freedom of Information Act to obtain records from Elon Musk's Department of Government Efficiency. CREW is seeking directives to federal agencies on firing workers and canceling contracts, along with sworn testimony from DOGE administrator Amy Gleason. The administration, meanwhile, claims that DOGE was just a presidential advisory body, making it exempt from public records laws. Solicitor General D. John Sauer argued that the ruling "intrudes into the autonomy and confidentiality of presidential advisors" and threatens future administrations with burdensome public records lawsuits. ([New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/13/us/politics/supreme-court-doge-musk-public-records.html) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/10/13/supreme-court-review-access-us-doge-service-records/) / [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/supreme-court-elon-musk-doge-e04914af1491ed395e54efc783e0f19d) / [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-13/supreme-court-to-consider-halting-bid-for-information-about-doge) / [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/13/politics/supreme-court-doge-asylum-plea-nigeria-terrorist-chef) / [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/supreme-court/supreme-court-to-weigh-doge-document-dispute-crew-rcna601585) / [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/supreme-court-doge-case/) / [Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/10/13/DOGE-supreme-court-case) / [The Hill](https://thehill.com/regulation/court-battles/6121673-supreme-court-doge-foia-review/))
