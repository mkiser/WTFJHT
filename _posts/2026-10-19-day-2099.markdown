1/ **The Justice Department charged 28 legal permanent residents in California with voting-related crimes and arrested 11**. All 28 appeared on a list of more than 20,000 voters the Trump administration claims are “likely noncitizens.” Acting Deputy Attorney General Trent McCotter accused California of “state-facilitated voter fraud,” and asked a federal judge to set aside the ballots from everyone on the list until their citizenship could be verified. California Secretary of State Shirley Weber, however, said the department provided no evidence supporting its list and didn't explain how it identified the voters. Los Angeles County election officials also refused to open a password-protected thumb drive containing the names, citing privacy and security protocols. California Attorney General Rob Bonta, meanwhile, called the 28 cases “a drop in the bucket” among the state’s 23 million registered voters. Noncitizen voting is extremely rare. ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/california-election-justice-department-60e3dd468f43e0e8a32e6bf69341f189) / [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/19/us/politics/doj-california-voting-crimes-trump-midterms.html) / [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-19/us-agents-arrest-11-in-california-over-alleged-illegal-voting) / [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-election-investigations-faith-in-voting-60-minutes/) / [Democracy Docket](https://www.democracydocket.com/news-alerts/trump-administration-reportedly-uses-list-of-20000-suspected-noncitizens-to-drive-arrests-in-california/))

2/ **Trump called a federal judge "a crazed lunatic" and declared that construction at East Potomac Golf Links would begin "IMMEDIATELY."** U.S. District Judge Ana Reyes questioned whether the Trump administration had misled the court about its plans to overhaul the public golf course, saying, "There is no universe in which all of this just coincidentally happens at the same time." Last week, Reyes barred further tree removal through Oct. 26 after the National Park Service cut down hundreds of trees with little notice. Reyes wrote: "Breakneck speed to clear trees that posed no harm. The reason? Disputed. The budget? Unclear. The environmental impact? Unstudied. The urgency? None. Next steps: bulldoze hundreds more." The Justice Department then sought to remove her from the case, citing hostility toward the government. Reyes denied the motion. ([Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/10/19/golf-course-hearing-donald-trump) / [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/19/us/politics/trump-washington-golf-course.html) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/10/19/trump-calls-judge-overseeing-east-potomac-park-legal-battle-crazed-lunatic/) / [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Politics/trump-attacks-judge-rejected-dojs-push-disqualify-dc/story?id=137376088) / [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/19/politics/trump-golf-course-renovation) / [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/trump-washington-courts-4a1ae5b321d7182d40039a41a7d00ba9) / [Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/us-news/trump-presses-ahead-with-plans-to-remake-washington-golf-course-27a26ba9) / [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/trump-administration/trump-announces-plans-renovate-dc-golf-course-legal-battle-rcna604147))

3/ **Trump signed an executive order creating a women's health division at the National Institutes of Health, as well as a White House task force**. The new division doesn't replace the NIH's existing Office of Research on Women's Health, which was established in 1990. Trump, nevertheless, appeared unaware of its existence, asking, "Who the hell wouldn't have a women's health division?" White House chief of staff Susie Wiles and Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. will lead the new task force. ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/melania-trump-womens-health-executive-order-47534d721ac1328832ca46d9286ca833) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/10/19/donald-melania-trump-announce-joint-effort-boost-womens-health/) / [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-executive-order-on-womens-health/) / [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/trump-administration/trump-melania-womens-health-nih-rcna604387))

4/ **Trump ordered an investigation into allegations that one of his top advisers promised presidential access in exchange for a Kennedy Center donation**. Ric Grenell allegedly promised Turkish billionaire Turhan Mildon “VIP events” and a chance to “get close with the administration, even with POTUS” in exchange for a donation.  Mildon claimed, at Grenell's direction, he wired $17.6 million to a Texas defense company, and that roughly $5.6 million was intended for the Kennedy Center, which Grenell was leading at the time. The Kennedy Center, however, never received the donation, and a White House official said the $17.6 million actually came from Congo's Finance Ministry.
