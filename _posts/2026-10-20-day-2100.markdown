3/ **Trump announced an $8 billion Agriculture Department bailout for farmers struggling with rising diesel prices caused by his war with Iran**. The one-time payments would cover part of farmers’ estimated losses this year, and follows a $12 billion bailout announced last December to offset losses from Trump's tariffs and trade policies. The Agriculture Department, however, doesn't have enough money in the designated account to fund the new payments. If funded, the bailout would bring the projected government payments to farmers this year to $55.4 billion, representing roughly a third of net farm income. Diesel prices, meanwhile, currently average $6.32 a gallon, up from $3.62 a year ago. ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/us-launch-8-billion-support-program-farmers-offset-diesel-costs-trump-says-2026-10-20/) / [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/20/business/trump-farmer-bailout.html) / [Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/10/20/trump-farmers-payments-gop-midterms-01128165) / [Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/politics/policy/trump-plans-8-billion-in-payments-to-farmers-1cec5851) / [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-farmer-harvest-fuel-relief-aid/) / [The Hill](https://thehill.com/business/agriculture/6160126-farmers-to-get-one-time-payments-from-8-billion-program-trump-says-what-to-know/))

4/ **Elon Musk reportedly ordered the slowdown of Ukraine's military-grade Starlink service**, which impacted some of its drones and weakened its ability to respond to Russian attacks. Musk claimed that limits on speed or usage were decided by the U.S. military and ultimately Trump. However, Trump said he didn't know who was responsible, while Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth said the Pentagon issued no directive and that Starlink could make decisions about its terms of service. Trump and Musk have criticized Ukraine for attacking Russian refineries, which Trump blames for rising diesel prices, although most analysts say the U.S. war with Iran has had a greater impact. ([Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/world/ukraine-starlink-elon-musk-trump-dbc49193) / [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/20/trump-musk-starlink-ukraine-zelenskyy.html) / [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/international/6161243-musk-ordered-starshield-slowdown/))

5/ **The Trump administration delayed shipments of Patriot interceptor missiles European allies already purchased for Ukraine** as Russia continues to attack Ukrainian cities and energy infrastructure. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth hasn't approved the transfers and the delay comes after Trump waived sanctions on Russian diesel exports and pressured Ukraine to stop attacking Russian refineries. The Trump administration has also reportedly threatened to suspend a NATO weapons-purchasing program unless Kyiv stopped the strikes. Secretary of State Marco Rubio, meanwhile, acknowledged that while some interceptors may not have been delivered, he insisted "there has not been some deliberate political directive to go out and harm Ukraine." Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy, however, responded: "If you cannot rein Putin in, then give us interceptor missiles so I can protect our energy infrastructure."
