1/ **House Judiciary Democrats called for an independent special counsel to investigate Trump’s ties to Jeffrey Epstein and allegations that the Justice Department covered up evidence of Trump's involvement in Epstein's sexual abuse of young women**. The 50-page staff report claimed Trump was "an active participant" in the abuse, citing accusations from five women who alleged that Trump raped or sexually assaulted them in Epstein’s company or presence. Democrats also alleged that Attorney General Todd Blanche, Trump's former personal attorney, may have obstructed justice in connection with his interview of Epstein accomplice Ghislaine Maxwell. The Justice Department called the report an effort to “mislead the public,” maintaining it had complied with the law requiring disclosure of the Epstein files. A federal judge, however, found in June that the department had failed to produce certain required records. Democrats, meanwhile, signaled that they would use their subpoena powers to pursue the investigation if they retake the House in next week's midterm elections. Only the attorney general can appoint a special counsel. 2/ **A federal judge ruled the Justice Department unlawfully fired Maurene Comey, who worked on the prosecutions of Jeffrey Epstein and Ghislaine Maxwell**. Comey alleged that she was fired either because she's the daughter of former FBI Director James Comey, or because of her perceived political beliefs. The Justice Department gave no explanation for her dismissal, but U.S. District Judge Jesse Furman found that her firing violated civil service protections and the constitutional separation of powers. Furman ordered Comey retroactively reinstated with back pay through Dec. 20, 2025, when she took another job. The Justice Department said it would appeal. 3/ **The average price of gasoline during Trump's second term is now more expensive than it was under Biden – by less than a cent**. Americans have paid an average of $3.472 a gallon under Trump, compared with $3.471 under Biden. The current national average, however, is $4.29 a gallon, up from $2.98 before Trump launched his war with Iran in February. Trump had promised to lower gasoline prices below $2 a gallon, but in September called higher fuel prices "a very inexpensive price to pay for what we've done." And, at a rally over the weekend, Trump celebrated the oil industry making "hundreds of billions of dollars" from the war, and claimed that his advisers had urged him not to mention it because "people are paying a little higher." Trump responded: "I don't give a damn. I'm going to say it. We're making a freaking fortune." Meanwhile, Democrats on Congress's Joint Economic Committee found that Trump's own oil and gas stocks increased by an average of 43% since the end of 2025. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-26/trump-s-gasoline-prices-surpass-biden-average-ahead-of-midterms) / [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/average-price-gasoline-during-trumps-term-now-exceeds-bidens-midterms-loom-2026-10-26/) / [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-oil-gas-stocks-gain-millions-iran-war-democratic-report-finds/) / [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/26/economy/gas-oil-trump-iran-election) / [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/trump-oil-gas-prices-midterms-iran-war-0ca3059bb35a0f70e15eb0ccb9c207c5))

The Justice Department wants the state to set aside ballots from voters from its list of 20,000 people it claims may be noncitizens until their eligibility can be verified. During an emergency hearing, however, the judges questioned the federal government’s authority to override a state election and why the department appealed before a lower court had ruled.
