1/ **Trump offered $1 million for voter fraud tips that lead to an arrest and conviction**, including the "past Elections, or the upcoming 2026 Midterm Election." Trump, however, didn't say where the money would come from, and the White House declined to provide additional details. There is no evidence of widespread voter fraud in U.S. elections, despite Trump's repeated false claims that fraud cost him the 2020 election. 2/ **A federal appeals court rejected the Trump administration's effort to force California to set aside 20,000 mail-in ballots from voters it claims are noncitizens**. The 9th Circuit said the Justice Department had failed to give a lower court judge an adequate opportunity to consider the case, calling the administration's "claimed emergency, at least in part, is a self-constructed one." Two of the judges questioned the federal government's authority to intervene in state elections. California officials, meanwhile, questioned the accuracy of the list, saying that 112 of 200 sampled registrations were either canceled or inactive, and many others appeared to belong to U.S. citizens. ([Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/10/26/california-election-ballots-justice-department-ruling) / [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/trump-administration-loses-push-block-california-votes-alleged-noncitizens-2026-10-26/) / [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/california-noncitizen-voting-justice-department-lawsuit-ed14390afb8af8fde1c2d0f6a90a0761) / [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/trump-administration-loses-push-block-california-votes-alleged-nonciti-rcna605835) / [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/26/politics/california-mail-ballot-noncitizen-appeals-court-rejects) / [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/appeals-court-dismisses-doj-trump-california-ballots/) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/10/26/california-ballots-targeted-by-trump-can-be-counted-now-under-court-ruling/) / [Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/politics/policy/court-rejects-trump-administration-bid-to-set-aside-california-mail-in-ballots-0772b320))

* **The Justice Department demanded that Washington state set ballots from 405 voters it claims are noncitizens**. Secretary of State Steve Hobbs said his office is reviewing the request, but noted that one person on the list last voted in 1994 and said "The timing of this request – one week before the General Election – is no doubt intended to cast unfounded suspicion on the results." ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/washington-noncitizen-voting-63c17a3eadc9ed232fbd897afbd29b9d) / [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/doj-asks-washington-state-set-400-ballots-voters-says-may-noncitizens-rcna605836) / [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/justice-department-washington-ballots-400-alleged-noncitizens/) / [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Politics/trump-admin-demands-washington-set-aside-400-ballots/story?id=137593785))

3/ **The Supreme Court refused to reinstate a Biden-era rule letting immigration judges temporarily close some deportation cases**. Texas sued to overturn the rule in June, and the Justice Department agreed the same day to a court order permanently blocking it nationwide. Three cities and an immigrant advocacy group called it “egregious collusion,” accusing the Trump administration of using “manufactured litigation” with an “ideologically aligned” state to eliminate the practice, known as "administrative closure." The rule had allowed judges to temporarily postpone deportation cases while immigrants pursued humanitarian protections or other legal status, without dismissing their cases. Because the cases are closed, they could now be reopened. ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/immigration-supreme-court-deportation-a9c226027bc8f0f65ceb4f4ab5ecacb4) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/10/27/trump-administration-can-block-immigration-judges-closing-deportation-cases/) / [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/27/us/politics/supreme-court-texas-immigration.html) / [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/supreme-court-declines-restore-policy-allowing-immigration-judges/story?id=137614314) / [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-27/supreme-court-allows-doj-deportation-deal-decried-as-collusion) / [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/us-supreme-court-denies-immigration-judges-power-shelve-deportation-cases-2026-10-27/))

4/ **A federal judge blocked the Trump administration from cutting down more trees at the East Potomac Golf Links**, and required 10 business days' notice before closing or irreversibly altering the park. U.S. District Judge Ana Reyes issued the preliminary injunction after roughly 430 trees were removed with less than a day's notice, rejecting the Trump administration's claim that it was routine maintenance unrelated to Trump's plan to turn the public course into a championship-level venue. Justice Department lawyers, however, insisted that no final renovation plan exists, even as Trump publicly announced that Interior Secretary Doug Burgum had developed one and construction would begin "IMMEDIATELY." The Justice Department, Reyes wrote, "asks the Court to disbelieve repeated public comments made by the President." Trump, meanwhile, said the judge had "absolutely no right to hold up this very important and beautiful Project," and said that the course would close Nov. 15. ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/10/27/nx-s1-6019192/east-potomac-golf-course-renovations-blocked-appeal) / [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/26/politics/judge-imposes-limits-trump-golf-course-renovation) / [Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/10/26/judge-bars-trump-admin-from-further-tree-felling-closures-at-golf-course-01135977) / [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/judge-trump-trees-dc-golf-course-lorax/) / [Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/politics/policy/judge-bars-trump-administration-from-cutting-more-trees-on-d-c-golf-course-54670223) / [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/trump-administration/judge-extends-block-trump-officials-removing-trees-dc-golf-course-east-rcna605852) / [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/26/us/politics/trump-golf-reyes-judge-ruling.html) / [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/trump-golf-course-east-potomac-f1fafe7da7796e24b2b0626bc9f9ba42) / [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Politics/wireStory/judge-extends-court-ordered-block-trumps-renovation-plans-137590006))

5/ **The FBI searched a Beverly Hills mansion tied to a former Eric Swalwell donor** as part of an investigation into sexual assault allegations against Swalwell. The FBI, however, confirmed only that it was executing a federal search warrant in an ongoing criminal investigation and declined to provide details. Swalwell resigned from Congress in April and dropped his run for California governor after the allegations. Agents also searched his Washington home and seized his electronic devices in August.
