### 🗞️ The week in WTF.

1/ **The Justice Department issued more than 40 grand jury subpoenas and record-preservation notices in its investigation of Alaska's Senate race**. The requests follow reports that aides to Democratic candidate Mary Peltola and national Democratic operatives helped recruit an independent candidate named Daniel J. Sullivan to draw votes from incumbent Republican Sen. Dan S. Sullivan. Peltola’s campaign denied involvement with either of the Sullivan campaigns. The Justice Department already sued to delay certification if the Republican incumbent narrowly loses and the votes for the other Sullivan could change the outcome. The Alaska Supreme Court, however, ruled that the independent candidate could remain on the ballot. Alaska Republican Gov. Mike Dunleavy ordered a state investigation into whether Democrats broke state law, but didn't identify what law may have been broken. Trump, meanwhile, claimed the alleged scheme was “voter fraud at the highest level,” demanded prosecutions, and declined to say whether Republicans should seat Peltola if she wins. ([Monday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/26/day-2106/) / [Thursday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/29/day-2109/))

2/ **The Justice Department subpoenaed Los Angeles County election workers to appear before a federal grand jury as part of a criminal investigation into alleged noncitizen voting**. The subpoenas require them to appear the day after the midterms and seeks records about how the county identifies and prevents noncitizen voting. A federal judge, meanwhile, rejected the administration’s request to set aside ballots from roughly 20,000 voters it claims are likely noncitizens, saying the federal government could “create chaos for their own political purpose to disrupt the election process.” The 9th Circuit had already rejected the department’s emergency appeal because it hadn't given the lower court an adequate chance to rule. California officials also questioned the list’s accuracy, saying 112 of 200 sampled registrations were canceled or inactive and many others appeared to belong to U.S. citizens. ([Monday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/26/day-2106/) / [Tuesday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/27/day-2107/) / [Thursday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/29/day-2109/))

3/ **Trump offered $1 million for voter fraud tips that lead to an arrest and conviction**, including tips about past elections and the 2026 midterms. Trump didn't say where the money would come from, and the White House declined to provide details. There is no evidence of widespread voter fraud in U.S. elections, despite Trump's repeated false claims that fraud cost him the 2020 election. ([Tuesday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/27/day-2107/))

4/ **Trump's war with Iran has cost $47.1 billion so far – up $3.5 billion in roughly six weeks**. About $29 billion of that is for replacing munitions, and the estimate excludes damage to U.S. bases in the Middle East. Trump said the war would end “Whenever I want it to end,” but that he requires a “total statement” that Iran would never have a nuclear weapon. The U.S., meanwhile, told several Gulf Arab countries to prepare for a possible short, intense military campaign after the Nov. 3 midterms. ([Monday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/26/day-2106/) / [Wednesday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/28/day-2108/) / [Thursday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/29/day-2109/))

5/ **The economy grew at a 3.4% annual rate in the third quarter**, its fastest pace in a year and up from 2.2% in the spring. Consumer spending rose 3.8%, while business investment outside housing climbed 12.7% amid a surge in AI spending. Inflation accelerated to 3.6% in September, above the Federal Reserve’s 2% target. And, the Fed held interest rates steady at 3.75% to 4%, even as Fed Chair Kevin Warsh acknowledged that “inflation is too high.” Investors, meanwhile, expect a rate increase in December. ([Monday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/26/day-2106/) / [Wednesday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/28/day-2108/) / [Thursday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/29/day-2109/))

### 📊 The Dept. of Numbers.

*A few figures that caught my eye this week.*

- **U.S. national debt reached 100% of GDP for the first time since 1946**. The $32.2 trillion in publicly held debt equaled 100.03% of GDP, up from 99.4% last fiscal year.

- **Hackers leaked a searchable database of more than 41,000 Homeland Security employees and contractors, including undercover ICE agents**. The records include names, work phone numbers, email addresses, supervisors, and internal access groups tied to undercover operations, wiretaps, facial recognition, and surveillance programs.

- **Trump joked that his promised $5,000 checks to Americans if Republicans retain control of Congress in the midterms would buy little more than “a hamburger and maybe a small dessert” at Mar-a-Lago**. “I’m not even sure you could buy that," Trump added, saying that his club is "not a good place if you’re price-conscious.”
