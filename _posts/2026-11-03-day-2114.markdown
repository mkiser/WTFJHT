1/ **Voters are deciding whether Republicans will keep control of Congress for the final two years of Trump's presidency**. Democrats are favored to win the House, which would give them control of congressional committees and the power to investigate Trump and his administration. The Senate, however, is less certain. Republicans hold a 53-47 majority, and Democrats need to flip four Republican-held seats while defending all their own, with closely watched races in North Carolina, Maine, Texas, Kansas, Alaska, and Iowa, as well as Michigan, where Democrats are defending a seat. Trump's approval rating stands at 32% as Americans weigh high prices, the war in Iran, and his immigration crackdown. Trump, however, claimed on Election Day that "Inflation is solved." More than 59 million Americans have voted early, surpassing the 46 million who voted early in 2022. Republican turnout on Election Day, however, could determine whether it's a "blue wave" or "blue tsunami." Trump nevertheless claimed, "If our people vote, we win in a landslide." The first polls close at 6 p.m. Eastern in parts of Kentucky and Indiana, but control of Congress might not be known for a few days, or longer. 2/ **The Justice Department sent about 1,000 election monitors to more than 120 jurisdictions across all 50 states – its largest monitoring operation ever**. Nearly two-thirds of the jurisdictions selected have consistently voted Democratic in recent presidential elections. The Biden administration monitored Democratic-leaning areas in roughly the same proportion during the 2022 midterms. Democrats and local election officials warned that the deployment could intimidate voters or interfere with voting. Monitors, however, would be unarmed, out of uniform, and have no arrest powers. ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/03/election-day-polls-doj-observers) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/11/03/justice-dept-election-monitors-overwhelmingly-deployed-democratic-locales/) / [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-03/judge-rejects-bid-to-limit-whom-doj-can-send-as-poll-monitors))

3/ **The U.S. military secretly carried out airstrikes on Iran's Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz on the eve of the midterm elections**. U.S. officials described the attack as defensive after Iranian forces fired at American aircraft near the strait. The strike wasn't initially publicly disclosed, and Trump previously promised to "not be attacking Iran at any time prior to the Midterm Elections." He called the strikes "just a minor attack" after claiming on Monday that "we haven't been doing any fighting for months." Trump, however, has repeatedly threatened to resume major attacks after the midterms.
