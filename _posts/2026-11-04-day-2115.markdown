1/ **Democrats won control of both the House and Senate in the midterm elections to take full control of Congress**. When the new Congress convenes in January, Democrats will gain control of congressional committees and subpoena powers, allowing them to investigate Trump and his administration during the final two years of his presidency.

2/ **Democrats flipped seven Republican-held seats in the Senate** – Texas, Iowa, Kansas, South Carolina, Ohio, Maine, and North Carolina – including several states Trump had won by double digits in 2024. Democrats also held on to competitive seats in Georgia, Michigan, and New Hampshire.

3/ **In the House, Democrats flipped more than two dozen Republican-held seats** across Pennsylvania, Iowa, Wisconsin, Kentucky, Missouri, Indiana, and North Carolina. They're projected to have their largest majority since 2009 with at least 258 seats.

4/ **Democrats also flipped five Republican-held governorships**, giving the party control of a majority of governors' offices for the first time since 2010. They won in Texas, Georgia, Ohio, Iowa, and Nevada – all states that Trump won in 2024 – while defending their governorships in Wisconsin, Arizona, Michigan, and Maine.
