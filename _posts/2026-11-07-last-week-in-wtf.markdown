### 🗞️ The week in WTF.

1/ **Democrats won both the House and Senate in the midterm elections to take full control of Congress after flipping seven Republican-held seats in the Senate and more than two dozen Republican-held seats in the House, as well as five Republican-held governorships**. They took the governor’s office and both legislative chambers in Michigan, Minnesota, Nevada, Pennsylvania, and Wisconsin, and won the Texas House for the first time since 2002. Democrats also won their first majority of governorships since 2010, while Republicans lost legislative supermajorities in several states, limiting their ability to override Democratic governors’ vetoes. Democrats could gain full control of Arizona, where a legislative race remains undecided. ([Monday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/02/day-2113/) / [Tuesday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/03/day-2114/) / [Thursday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/05/day-2116/))

2/ **The White House warned conservative groups it was “monitoring statements, press releases, and media engagements” from their organizations**. The memo reportedly went to more than 3,000 groups after Republicans lost control of the House and Senate, though the White House denied it was a threat. Ted Cruz called the losses a “Watergate-level repudiation,” saying Trump’s tariffs “have made everything too expensive” and the Iran war “has dragged on too long.” JD Vance, meanwhile, said “The reason we lost is the war,” which Trump called “largely true,” but downplayed the war’s impact and warned Democrats that “I have total veto power, so they have to come to us.” ([Monday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/02/day-2113/) / [Wednesday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/04/day-2115/) / [Thursday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/05/day-2116/))

3/ **Senate Democratic leader Chuck Schumer vowed to prevent Trump from putting "a right-winger" on the Supreme Court** and warned that Democrats would block his lower-court and Cabinet nominees when they take control of the Senate on Jan. 3. The midterm results increase pressure on conservative Justices Clarence Thomas, 78, and Samuel Alito, 76, to retire before then. Alito, however, said he doesn't plan to retire immediately, while Thomas has previously ruled out stepping down. ([Thursday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/05/day-2116/))

4/ **Rep. Thomas Massie pledged to force a House vote next week to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth**. Massie accused Hegseth of waging the Iran war without congressional authorization and said he would drop the effort only if Hegseth resigned. Hegseth called the impeachment articles a “sham,” while Trump publicly defended him but reportedly asked advisers whether he should keep his job. The threat follows U.S. airstrikes on Iran’s Qeshm Island on Nov. 2, which U.S. officials described as defensive after Iranian forces fired at American aircraft near the Strait of Hormuz despite Trump’s promise not to attack Iran before the midterm elections. ([Tuesday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/03/day-2114/) / [Thursday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/05/day-2116/))

* **Trump said he'll soon decide whether to "make a deal" with Iran or "finish it off."** ([Monday, Oct. 26](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/26/day-2106/))
* **Trump's war with Iran has cost $47.1 billion so far – up $3.5 billion in roughly six weeks**. ([Wednesday, Oct. 28](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/28/day-2108/))
* **Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth fired the Pentagon’s top military planner over allegations that he leaked classified information to journalists**. ([Thursday, Oct. 29](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/10/29/day-2109/))

5/ **House Democrats called for Rep. Haley Stevens to be expelled from their caucus** after she endorsed Republican Mike Rogers over Democratic Senate nominee Abdul El-Sayed. Stevens, who lost the Democratic primary to El-Sayed, had promised to support him as recently as Oct. 31. Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer called the endorsement a “despicable betrayal,” and Michigan Democratic Party Chair Curtis Hertel said Stevens would be ineligible for future membership in the state party. Several of Stevens’ staffers resigned, including her longtime chief of staff. El-Sayed defeated Rogers, becoming the first Muslim American elected to the U.S. Senate. ([Monday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/02/day-2113/))

6/ **House Speaker Mike Johnson doesn't plan to stay on as the Republican leader after Democrats won the House**. House Majority Leader Steve Scalise and Judiciary Committee Chair Jim Jordan said they plan to run for minority leader. ([Wednesday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/04/day-2115/))

### ⏰ Dept. of What Just Happened Since My Last Update.

1. **Trump named Stephen Miller his new White House chief of staff, replacing Susie Wiles**. Miller, Trump's deputy chief of staff and the architect of his mass-deportation campaign, takes over Monday. Wiles, the first woman to hold the job and Trump's longest-serving chief of staff, will move to Trump's political operation. When asked why he replaced her, Trump said that "Sometimes in life you have to make moves." Miller helped develop Trump's family-separation policy and the 2017 travel ban during Trump's first term, and has overseen his second term's immigration crackdown. About six in 10 midterm voters said the administration's immigration enforcement had gone too far, and some Republicans blamed Miller for the Republicans' midterm losses. After immigration agents killed two U.S. citizens in Minneapolis, Miller called one of them a "domestic terrorist" and later blamed the comment on bad information from Customs and Border Protection. Meanwhile, Democratic Rep. Robert Garcia, who is set to lead the House Oversight Committee, vowed to investigate Miller and warned: "Stephen Miller should lawyer up." ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/susie-wiles-trump-miller-midterms-95690fe076f6bb8cfe2bf77435a1910d) / [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/live/2026/11/06/us/trump-miller-wiles-chief-of-staff) / [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/donald-trump/trump-stephen-miller-susie-wiles-white-house-chief-of-staff-rcna607892) / [Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/11/06/susie-wiles-stephen-miller-trump-chief-of-staff-01154493) / [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-06/trump-taps-stephen-miller-to-replace-wiles-as-chief-of-staff) / [Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/politics/elections/trump-names-stephen-miller-his-new-chief-of-staff-2f835632) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/11/06/trump-names-stephen-miller-new-white-house-chief-staff/))

National Security Division chief John Eisenberg and Civil Rights Division chief Harmeet Dhillon also resigned. The investigation produced no charges.
