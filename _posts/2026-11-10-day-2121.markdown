1/ **The Republican-led House Oversight Committee accused the Trump administration of mishandling the Epstein files** and called on the Justice Department to reinterview key witnesses and investigate allegations against Epstein’s associates. The committee's report blamed decades of failures by law enforcement and federal prosecutors for allowing Epstein’s abuse to continue. “Epstein operated with impunity, shielded and enabled by powerful connections and a justice system that repeatedly failed his victims,” Chairman James Comer said. The report noted that the department missed disclosure deadlines, exposed victims’ information through deficient redactions, concealed names of alleged co-conspirators, and had not given survivors access to their own records. Republicans, however, concluded that there was no evidence implicating Trump in Epstein's crimes. Meanwhile, the committee’s top Democrat, Robert Garcia, called the report “based on an incomplete investigation and zero public hearings on Jeffrey Epstein,” claimed the department was illegally withholding more than 2.5 million pages of records, and vowed to continue the investigation when Democrats take control of the House in January, saying "Our work is just getting started." 2/ **House Republicans blocked an effort to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth**, sending the charges to committee and effectively killing it for the remainder of the Republican-controlled Congress. Rep. Thomas Massie, the only Republican to join Democrats to oppose the move, accused Hegseth of waging war in Iran without congressional authorization, dismantling safeguards against civilian casualties, directing unlawful strikes on suspected drug boats, and retaliating against Democratic Sen. Mark Kelly. Hegseth, however, dismissed the charges as "partisan policy disagreements" and called the impeachment effort "sabotage," adding: "I was proud to execute them, and I would do it again tomorrow." 3/ **The U.S. military evacuated 13 aircraft from a second British air base following a reported threat from Iran**. Flight-tracking data showed 10 refueling tankers and three Ospreys departed RAF Mildenhall as security at the base and nearby RAF Lakenheath rose to FPCON Charlie – the military’s second-highest alert level, which indicates that a terrorist attack is considered likely. Last month, the military withdrew bombers from RAF Fairford over a suspected Iran-linked plot. Trump, meanwhile, said renewed military action against Iran “doesn’t seem to be a very necessary thing” at this point. 4/ **British police arrested a senior U.S. Army soldier on suspicion of spying for Russia**. The 41-year-old was assigned to NATO's Allied Rapid Reaction Corps near Gloucester, and was suspected of trying to steal secret information from NATO for Russia. However, officials said no sensitive information was passed to Russia. The unnamed soldier remains in custody at a London police station after being arrested under Britain's National Security Act. FBI Director Kash Patel said the arrest "involves allegations of espionage, which is of the utmost concern to the United States and our allies."
