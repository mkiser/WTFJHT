1/ **U.S. intelligence uncovered a possible Iranian plan to target senior Trump administration officials in Washington with a dirty bomb or chemical weapon**. The intelligence reportedly included specific locations and targets. The White House, however, said there's "no credible threats to the homeland," while Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin described Iran's planning as "aspirational." Trump, meanwhile, said the administration was taking the threat "very seriously," but warned that Iran would "pay a price the likes of which no country has ever paid before" if it attacked the U.S. ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/16/politics/us-intelligence-iran-dirty-bomb) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2026/11/17/trump-says-government-takes-report-iran-planning-dirty-bomb-very-seriously/) / [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/17/trump-iran-dirty-bomb-washington.html) / [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/17/trump-reported-iranian-plot-dirty-bomb-washington) / [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/17/dirty-bomb-radioactive-trump-washington-weapon))

2/ **The National Security Agency's classified internal communications network went offline, disrupting access to top-secret information**. The NSA said the outage was due to "scheduled routine maintenance" and that "mission-critical capabilities remain fully operational." The agency didn't explain the full scope of the disruption, but officials reported that there was no evidence of a cyberattack. Trump, meanwhile, dismissed the disruptions, saying, "It's life, it's life. When you're big and powerful and you have all the money, things like that happen. It's fine." 3/ **Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin took a government jet to a college football game a day after the FAA ordered a nationwide ground stop**. Mullin flew to Louisiana on Saturday to watch LSU play Texas in a private suite belonging to Ben Bordelon, CEO of Bollinger Shipyards, which holds billions of dollars in Department of Homeland Security contracts. DHS defended the trip, noting that Mullin is required to use government aircraft for security reasons and pays for his personal travel. "We would have never went if the airways weren't completely open," Mullin said, noting that the ground stop had ended 21 hours earlier, even as thousands of travelers remained stranded. "And we still gotta live our lives at the same time." ([New York Post](https://nypost.com/2026/11/17/us-news/markwayne-mullin-took-his-government-plane-to-lsu-texas-game-as-thousands-were-stranded-in-faa-ground-stoppage/) / [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/markwayne-mullin-nationwide-ground-stop-lsu-1155f7fe532f55b272d3b9fefcd68d2b) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/11/17/homeland-security-secretary-markwayne-mullin-defends-football-flight/) / [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2026/11/17/markwayne-mullin-flew-football-game-faa-ground-stop/92826005007/) / [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/nov/17/markwayne-mullin-private-jet-football) / [New Republic](https://newrepublic.com/post/218073/mullin-flew-football-game-flights-grounded))

4/ **Federal Reserve governor Lisa Cook refused to participate in a White House hearing on whether Trump can fire her**. The hearing is part of Trump's second attempt to remove Cook over mortgage-fraud allegations, after the Supreme Court ruled she must be given notice and a chance to respond. Cook has denied wrongdoing and hasn't been charged with a crime. Her lawyer accused the White House of trying to "manufacture 'cause' for a predetermined removal outcome." The White House, meanwhile, said the process would be fair, and warned that the committee could "draw adverse inferences" from her absence. ([Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/politics/policy/feds-lisa-cook-rebuffs-white-house-hearing-citing-biased-panel-e6eac42b) / [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/lisa-cook-federal-reserve-white-house-hearing/) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/11/17/fed-official-lisa-cook-refuses-attend-white-house-firing-hearing-citing-bias/) / [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/17/business/economy/fed-governor-lisa-cook-trump-white-house-hearing.html) / [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/17/lisa-cook-hearing-fed-white-house))

5/ ***Well, that’s fantastic***: **The Commodity Futures Trading Commission ordered Kalshi to stop bets on what Trump might say in public**. The CFTC said it was reviewing whether the contracts are susceptible to manipulation and that Kalshi “has not attempted to establish that the Contracts are non-manipulable.” Kalshi CEO Tarek Mansour, however, disagreed, arguing that Trump’s remarks move markets. The order follows a settlement with a former White House teleprompter operator who paid a $65,000 penalty and returned more than $107,000 in profits over allegations that he used advance access to Trump’s speeches to trade on Kalshi.
