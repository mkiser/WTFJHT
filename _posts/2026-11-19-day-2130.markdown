1/ **Senate Republicans blocked a House-passed resolution directing Trump to end the Iran war or seek congressional approval**. The measure passed the House in September, but failed 51-48 in the Senate. It was the first congressional vote on the war since Republicans lost control of both chambers in the midterms. About six in 10 voters considered the U.S. decision to attack Iran wrong. JD Vance also blamed the war for Republicans’ losses in the midterms, saying: “The reason we lost is the war.” The Pentagon, meanwhile, estimates that the war had cost at least $48.3 billion as of Nov. 13. 2/ **Trump, Xi, and Putin ended their first three-way summit with a "peace declaration," but no agreements on reducing nuclear arsenals or ending the wars in Ukraine and Iran**. In their joint statement, the three said "a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought," pledged a dialogue on reducing nuclear risks, urged respect for the immunities of state officials, and warned the International Criminal Court against abusing its jurisdiction. They also pledged to increase fuel supplies as the U.S. war with Iran disrupted global oil markets. The statement, however, made no mention of the wars in Ukraine or Iran. Putin, who faces an ICC arrest warrant over alleged war crimes in Ukraine, told reporters afterward: "We will not negotiate after the attacks on the Crimean bridge," referring to a Ukrainian strike on the bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea. Trump, meanwhile, called the meeting a "12" on a scale of zero to 10. ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-xi-putin-china-apec-summit/) / [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/19/politics/trump-putin-xi-meeting-takeaways) / [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/19/world/asia/trump-xi-putin.html) / [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/11/19/white-house-offers-little-information-summit-outcome/) / [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Politics/trump-china-trip-iran-ukraine-peace-declaration-takeaways/story?id=138292154) / [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-19/big-trump-xi-putin-power-play-delivers-more-style-than-substance) / [Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/world/trump-xi-and-putin-convene-first-ever-summit-as-world-order-shifts-82e58aab) / [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/trumps-historic-meeting-with-xi-putin-headlines-asia-trip-2026-11-18/) / [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/trump-xi-putin-china-apec-25e17ec8d9c55fbff960d5631eaba71d))

3/ **Elon Musk said he's "not MAGA" and regrets spending more than $400 million on Republicans in the midterms after Democrats won control of Congress**. "I feel pretty foolish about having spent so much money and not seeing much for it," Musk said. "If I could rewind the clock, I would have spent less money." Musk said his goal of preserving a Republican Senate majority "failed," called himself "ideologically quite moderate" with no "blind adherence" to Republican policies. He added: "I'm not sure what MAGA means." In 2024, however, Musk declared himself "dark gothic MAGA." Meanwhile, Trump called him "a friend of mine," but warned that "when people veer from it, they don't do very well." Bill Essayli will also examine special counsel Jack Smith’s classified documents investigation, which led to criminal charges against Trump. The assignment is part of a broader investigation into an alleged “grand conspiracy” by Obama- and Biden-era officials to undermine Trump. That investigation, however, has stalled due to a lack of evidence, multiple resignations, and no indictments. 5/ **Trump's nominee to lead the Government Accountability Office promised to investigate the White House where he currently works**. Eric Ueland, deputy director of the White House Office of Management and Budget, is seeking a 15-year term leading the Government Accountability Office, which has repeatedly found that the Trump administration has violated federal spending laws by withholding money approved by Congress. "I'm not here as a plant, a stooge, a lackey for anybody," Ueland said during his confirmation hearing. He declined, however, to say whether he agreed with the GAO's findings, and Trump is pushing to confirm Ueland before Democrats take control of the Senate in January.
