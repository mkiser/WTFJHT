### 🗞️ The Week in WTF.

1/ **House Democrats opened an investigation into whether Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick delayed restrictions on Nvidia chip exports to benefit his sons' investment firm**. Democrats demanded records from Cantor Fitzgerald, which Lutnick previously ran and his sons now control, about its Nvidia holdings, which were valued at about $1.2 billion when he took office, and communications with the Commerce Department about export controls. Commerce, meanwhile, issued a rule requiring licenses to ship advanced chips to data centers in five countries to prevent Chinese companies from accessing them overseas. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said several agencies had pushed for months to blacklist Megaspeed, a Singapore-based Nvidia customer under investigation for allegedly diverting chips to China, while Lutnick reportedly resisted restrictions on the company. Trump said he had no plans to fire Lutnick and suggested the delay in blacklisting Megaspeed was meant to avoid a “blow-up with China” ahead of his meeting with Xi Jinping. ([Monday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/16/day-2127/) / [Wednesday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/18/day-2129/) / [Wednesday, Nov. 11](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/11/day-2122/))

* **Trump said the FBI was looking into Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s affair with a suspected Chinese spy**. ([Monday, Nov. 9](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/09/day-2120/))
* **Trump suggested that former Apple CEO Tim Cook take over Nvidia after Jensen Huang abruptly resigned following reports that he had affairs with a suspected Chinese spy, an executive who reported to him, and a second employee**. ([Thursday, Nov. 12](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/12/day-2123/))

2/ **Trump, Xi, and Putin ended their first three-way summit with a "peace declaration," but no agreements on reducing nuclear arsenals or ending the wars in Ukraine and Iran**. Their joint statement said “a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought” and pledged dialogue on reducing nuclear risks, but made no mention of either war. It also urged respect for the immunities of state officials and warned the International Criminal Court against abusing its jurisdiction. Putin faces an ICC arrest warrant over alleged war crimes in Ukraine. Trump rated the meeting a “12” on a scale of zero to 10. ([Wednesday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/18/day-2129/) / [Thursday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/19/day-2130/))

3/ **Senate Republicans blocked a House-passed resolution directing Trump to end the Iran war or seek congressional approval**. The measure failed 51-48 in the first congressional vote on the war since Republicans lost both chambers in the midterms. The Pentagon estimates that the war had cost at least $48.3 billion as of Nov. 13, while about six in 10 voters considered the U.S. decision to attack Iran wrong. JD Vance, meanwhile, blamed Republicans’ midterm losses on the war, saying: “The reason we lost is the war.” On Nov. 12, Trump pulled Vance out of negotiations to end it, saying, “We don’t need a deal with Iran.” ([Thursday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/19/day-2130/) / [Thursday, Nov. 12](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/12/day-2123/))

* **House Republicans blocked an effort to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth** ([Tuesday, Nov. 10](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/10/day-2121/))
* **71% of Americans want Congress to pass legislation to end Trump's war with Iran** ([Tuesday, Nov. 10](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/10/day-2121/))

4/ **House Democrats nominated Hakeem Jeffries for speaker, putting him on track to become the first Black speaker of the House**. The full House must still elect him on Jan. 3, when Democrats will have one of the biggest incoming majorities in decades. Seven newly elected democratic socialist lawmakers voted against Jeffries, saying their constituents had sent them to Congress with a mandate for change on issues including Medicare for All, ICE, and Gaza. Jeffries replied: “Our job is to fight MAGA extremism – not each other.” ([Wednesday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/18/day-2129/))

* **House Republicans elected Jim Jordan as their new leader**. ([Tuesday, Nov. 10](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/10/day-2121/))
* **Democrat Scott Colom defeated Republican Sen. Cindy Hyde-Smith in Mississippi**. ([Thursday, Nov. 12](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/12/day-2123/))

5/ **House Democrats directed members of Trump's family, JD Vance, and top administration officials to preserve records for House Oversight investigations**. Rep. Robert Garcia, who is expected to chair the committee, sent 12 letters ahead of planned investigations into alleged conflicts of interest involving Trump family businesses, misconduct by immigration officials, and the administration’s handling of the Jeffrey Epstein files. The White House said it wouldn’t comply with the requests before January, when Democrats take control of the House and gain subpoena power. The White House also instructed all executive agencies to get its approval before allowing employees to testify before Congress. ([Monday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/16/day-2127/) / [Wednesday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/18/day-2129/))

* **Trump ordered the Justice Department to investigate Democrats for "threatening" Republicans with investigations after winning control of Congress in the midterms**. ([Monday, Nov. 9](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/09/day-2120/))
* **The Republican-led House Oversight Committee accused the Trump administration of mishandling the Epstein files** ([Tuesday, Nov. 10](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/10/day-2121/))
* **White House counsel Will Scharf threatened criminal investigations of Democrats over their planned oversight of the Trump administration**. ([Wednesday, Nov. 11](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/11/day-2122/))

6/ **U.S. intelligence uncovered a possible Iranian plan to target senior Trump administration officials in Washington with a dirty bomb or chemical weapon**. The intelligence reportedly included specific locations and targets, but the White House said there were “no credible threats to the homeland,” and Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin described Iran’s planning as “aspirational.” Trump said the administration was taking the threat “very seriously” and warned that Iran would “pay a price the likes of which no country has ever paid before” if it attacked the U.S. ([Tuesday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/17/day-2128/))

* **The U.S. military evacuated 13 aircraft from a second British air base following a reported threat from Iran**. ([Tuesday, Nov. 10](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/10/day-2121/))
* **Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent moved from his home to a military base for his safety following threats from Iran**. ([Wednesday, Nov. 11](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/11/day-2122/))

7/ **Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin took a government jet to a college football game a day after the FAA ordered a nationwide ground stop**. The four-hour ground stop disrupted thousands of flights and affected hundreds of thousands of passengers. Mullin flew to Louisiana on Saturday to watch LSU play Texas from a private suite belonging to Ben Bordelon, whose shipbuilding company holds billions of dollars in DHS contracts, while thousands of travelers remained stranded. DHS said Mullin is required to use government aircraft for security reasons and pays for personal travel. “We would have never went if the airways weren’t completely open,” Mullin said. “And we still gotta live our lives at the same time.” ([Monday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/16/day-2127/) / [Tuesday](https://whatthefuckjusthappenedtoday.com/2026/11/17/day-2128/))

### ⏰ The Dept. of WTF Just Happened.

*A few updates since my last, well, update.*

1. **Trump ordered federal contractors to notify the White House of congressional requests for information and to withhold any material they deem confidential**. The order also covers subcontractors and information the administration considers protected by executive privilege. The order comes as Democrats are preparing to take control of Congress in January and plan to investigate the administration. Companies that fail to comply could lose existing contracts or be suspended from future federal work. ([Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/11/21/trump-white-house-orders-contractors-limit-information-shared-with-congress/) / [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/administration/6235181-trump-executive-privilege-congress-investigations-order/) / [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/21/trump-orders-federal-contractors-to-notify-white-house-before-giving-congress-information.html))

2. **The FBI reportedly opened an investigation into an apparent hack of its National Crime Information Center**, which federal, state, and local law enforcement agencies use to share information about fugitives, outstanding warrants, missing persons, criminal histories, and stolen property. The scope of the hack and whether any data was taken is unclear. Trump said the breach was "not good," but suggested it might reveal information about what he called the "Russia, Russia, Russia hoax" and, without evidence, "all the voter fraud that took place." He added, "Maybe we'll learn things that we would have never learned otherwise," because "I always like to put things in a positive spin." ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/justice-department/fbi-investigating-potential-breach-crime-database-rcna610536) / [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/20/politics/fbi-hacked-again-target-crime-database) / [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/fbi-database-ncic-hack-d066cff87a64a564db0a00172c124972) / [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/21/trump-russia-fbi-hack) / [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/21/trump-fbi-database-hack.html) / [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/technology/6235094-fbi-investigates-ncic-breach/))

3. **White House chief of staff Stephen Miller reportedly pushed out the nation's top cybersecurity official**. The move follows a series of tech outages that disrupted Microsoft services, major banks, the nationwide air traffic control system, and a classified National Security Agency network, which Trump downplayed as unrelated "individual events," telling Americans to "relax." Sean Cairncross didn't have a technical background, but was responsible for the administration's cybersecurity strategy and helped shape its artificial intelligence policies. Trump named Pentagon chief information officer Kirsten Davies to replace Cairncross in an acting capacity, pending Senate confirmation. ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/19/trump-national-cyber-director-sean-cairncross-leaving) / [Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/11/19/sean-cairncross-cyber-director-out-01177151) / [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/19/us/politics/white-house-cyber-director-departs.html) / [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/white-house-national-cyber-director-sean-cairncross-leaving/) / [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/19/politics/national-cyber-director-replaced-sean-cairncross) / [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/cyber-director-kirsten-davies-sean-cairncross-2f1f84f3ca5429d01ace67929c72128d) / [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-20/white-house-dismisses-trump-s-cyber-security-chief-cairncross) / [Mediaite](https://www.mediaite.com/politics/stephen-miller-reportedly-pushed-out-top-cybersecurity-official-for-lacking-expertise/))

Asked whether Putin was also a friend to America after Russia attacked Ukraine following their meeting, Trump replied, "I can't speak to America." During a White House news conference, Trump was asked whether Putin was a friend to America after Russia attacked Ukraine following their meeting in China. He replied: "I can't speak to America." Trump then introduced Miller as "the most powerful man in the world," declared that he was "better than these two guys," referring to JD Vance and Secretary of State Marco Rubio standing behind him, both potential 2028 presidential contenders, and asked, "Do you want to run for president?"
