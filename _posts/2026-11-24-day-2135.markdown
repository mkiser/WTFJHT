1/ **Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO after Trump "convinced them."** Huang resigned 11 days earlier over an affair with a suspected Chinese spy. Nvidia's board said last week it did "not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO." Nevertheless, Trump and JD Vance reportedly lobbied the board and assured directors that Huang wasn't a national security risk. "I made the deal," Trump said, adding that last week's stock market sell-off also "probably" influenced his decision. "We have to protect our geniuses," Trump added. Trump also claimed that Attorney General Todd Blanche confirmed the government "does not possess evidence that he has engaged in any unlawful conduct." The Justice Department, however, is still investigating whether Megaspeed, the Singapore-based company where the woman was an executive, illegally diverted advanced Nvidia chips to China. The Commerce Department blacklisted Megaspeed on Monday. 2/ **Trump's personal investment in Nvidia could have gained as much as $15 million in value after he pressured the company's board to reinstate CEO Jensen Huang**. Nvidia shares jumped 31.5% on Monday after its board restored Huang as CEO. Financial disclosures showed Trump owned at least $12 million in Nvidia stock at the end of 2025 and that his accounts continued trading it through September, though his current stake is unclear. Trump also benefited from a rebound in Alphabet, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, and Tesla shares, all of which he owns. The White House, meanwhile, maintained that Trump's investments are "independently managed by third-party financial institutions" and denied that he has any influence over investment decisions. 5/ **The COP31 climate summit ended without an agreement after the European Union and other countries rejected a draft that cut all references to fossil fuels**. Saudi Arabia and Russia opposed including the language, even though countries agreed in 2023 to "transition away" from fossil fuels. The U.S., one of the largest emitters of greenhouse gases, didn't send a delegation, and the collapse was the first failure to reach a final agreement at U.N. climate talks since 2009.
