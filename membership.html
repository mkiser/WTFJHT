Who The F*ck Supports This?

You do. It's not enough to be a consumer of media. You must be a stakeholder in it.

WTFJHT is a free, independent, reader-funded newsletter produced daily by Matt Kiser – a real life human! – since 2017. If this once-a-day briefing helps you make sense of the news, then consider becoming a supporting member so it can stay free for everyone who needs it. Here's the deal: This is my full-time job, made possible entirely by readers who choose to underwrite it so everyone else can read for free – and I plan to keep doing it for as long as you keep supporting me. This model keeps WTFJHT free while funding a fair living wage and the tools and systems that make publishing possible. That’s what makes membership an investment, rather than a charity or subscription. Because membership isn't about buying access for yourself, it's about unlocking access for others.

How I know this model works: My ability to fund WTFJHT is a direct reflection of the value created for readers. If not enough readers find WTFJHT valuable enough to pay for, the model stops working, and maybe WTFJHT shouldn't exist anymore. And yet... in a typical month, about 99% of supporting members stick around and choose to continue supporting WTFJHT. I think that's a pretty good sign that this thing is earning its keep.

So if you rely on WTFJHT for its clear, consistent, and honest telling of what happened today, then invest in its continued production by becoming a supporting member.

How the math maths: About 2% of readers underwrite WTFJHT, which unlocks free access for everyone else. While delivering the newsletter is cheap, it's not free. It costs about $2.09 per subscriber per year to produce, publish, and send WTFJHT. At the current average contribution of $5.93/month, each member contributes the equivalent of the annual cost of delivering WTFJHT to about 34 readers.

Where your $5.93/mo. contribution goes: Roughly speaking, $0.77 goes to unavoidable credit card processing and platform fees (roughly 13% of revenue!!!); $0.49 covers the operating costs and tools needed to publish and send the newsletter; $2.90 pays for my labor, which includes reporting, writing, editing, maintaining the site, and running the business; and the remaining $1.77 is the cushion that lets me absorb all the normal, it’s-not-you-it’s-me reasons people cancel without cutting back or interrupting the daily work with (annoying) membership drives.

Said another way: $5.93 moves the needle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I send you a check? Yes! My physical address is: WTF Just Happened Today? 505 Broadway E #211, Seattle, WA 98102 USA

Are you a 501(c)(3)? Is my contribution tax-deductible? No, I'm a for-profit, single-member LLC based in Washington State. There are two reasons for this: 1) this setup makes it easy to manage and keeps my operational overhead low, and 2) I strongly believe media businesses need to be in the business of being in business.

How much does this thing cost to run anyway? Glad you asked! I did some back of the envelope math and I came up with a baseline estimate of $16,778/month. If you're interested in how I arrived at that number, I've outlined the cost of running WTFJHT in the FAQ.

How do I login to my membership account? If you're on the Memberful platform (most members are), you can sign-in here. If you're on DonorBox, this is your sign-in page. And, if you're a Patreon member, here you go.

How do I update my credit card? Log in to your account, add your new card and click Update my credit card.

Can I update my email address or other personal information? Yes. Log in to your account here to make these changes.

How do I cancel my membership? For Memberful members, log in to your account and toggle auto-renew off from the "Subscriptions" menu item. For a legacy Patreon, PayPal, or Donorbox contribution: cancel it through the platform named on the charge or original receipt; the current membership login may not control legacy contributions.

Does unsubscribing from the newsletter automatically cancel my recurring membership? No. If you'd like to cancel or edit your recurring membership, please use the links provided in your receipt emails (or the appropriate link above) to manage your membership. Because WTFJHT is free but supported by optional contributions, it's not possible to make the assumption that someone unsubscribing from the newsletter also wants to cancel their patronage. In many cases, supporting members do not and have never been newsletter subscribers (e.g. a blog reader or RSS followers). For these reasons, I provide members with the tools to manage their account at any time for any reason.

Not sure how to access your membership account? Search your inbox for the receipt (the sender names the platform) or read the description next to the charge on your statement. Still stuck? Email me with any addresses you may have used and I'll find it. Paid through a legacy platform? Manage it there: Patreon · Donorbox · PayPal.

Can I change my membership amount or billing interval? Yes. Sign in to your membership account and change your level of support at any time.

Can I pause my support instead of canceling it? On the current WTFJHT membership there is no pause button, but if you email me, I can move your next renewal to a later date. Donorbox has a pause in its portal; Patreon and PayPal do not, so cancel renewal and rejoin when you're ready.

Should I send my credit-card number by email? No. Never send me your password, full card number, expiration date, or security code! Email me with whatever questions you have and I'll provide you a secure link for accessing your membership account.
