It's not enough to be a consumer of media. You must be a stakeholder in it.

WTFJHT is free for everyone, but supported entirely by an optional, membership model where you don’t get anything other than the satisfaction of supporting something you’d like to see exist in the world. About 2% of readers underwrite WTFJHT, which unlocks free access for everyone else.

And that’s what makes membership an investment, rather than a charity or subscription. Because membership isn’t about buying access for yourself, it’s about unlocking access for others.

Manage support at any time using the link in your receipt from Memberful, or sign-in to your membership account here.

Questions or comments? Just let me know.

I'm glad you're here.
