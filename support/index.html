Not sure which one charged you?

A few quick checks usually settle it — the receipt sender, the description next to the charge, and which email you used. Walk through the checks, or send a safe support request and I'll help.

Browse the full support FAQ

The newsletter is free and always will be. To start or stop the free daily email, use the subscribe page or the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any edition. That's separate from membership — unsubscribing does not cancel a payment, and canceling a payment does not stop the newsletter.
